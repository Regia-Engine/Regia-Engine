# v0.1.13.1 (hotfix on v0.1.13)

- `ferrite_render`: a draw whose mesh, object or material entry is gone no longer panics a render task (`expect("object entry")` and the
  sibling expects, 17 draw sites in `frame.rs`). The draw is skipped and a `warn` is logged once per kind and id.
- Seen by DO27: a panic in a TaskPool thread during `render_windows`, 20 s to 6 min into play, and in every run with 10 skinned AI wingmen.
- Candidate causes, not proven: the end-of-frame sweep (`objects.retain`) and the skin-palette growth clear (`objects.clear()`).
  The root cause is still being hunted; this tag removes the crash, not the cause. Nothing else changed. The workspace version stays 0.1.13.
