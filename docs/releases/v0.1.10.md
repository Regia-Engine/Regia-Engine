# Ferrite v0.1.10

Patch release for Direct Order 27's alpha build (Thursday 10-15), made on `release/0.1` from v0.1.9. It fixes the two
problems the v0.1.8 integration smoke found: pipelines still compiled after START (stutter in the first seconds of a
match), and a quit that could take close to 3 s on a big map at 4K.

- **Commit:** v0.1.10 = the tag `v0.1.10` (the gated code commit is named in the tag message).
- **Workspace version:** `0.1.10`.
- **Branch:** `release/0.1` (alpha hotfixes). `main` is the post-alpha development line (batch v020); this release is merged forward into it.

## Upgrade from v0.1.9
No host source change (checked from the code diff and DO27's `shell-ferrite`: no literal `GameFrameWarm { .. }` or
`GpuExitReport { .. }`). New public fields: `GameFrameWarm::progress_grace` and `max_timeout` (with defaults; a literal
needs `..Default::default()`), `GpuExitReport::renderer_dropped` (engine-filled). Everything else is additive.
Host checklist: "Warm coverage (v0.1.10)" and "Quit timing and the game-warm watchdog (v0.1.10)".

## Warm coverage (ADR-195)
A DO27-like session (`samples/lookdev/tests/warm_coverage.rs`: boot warm, level load, game-frame warm, 120 frames of play
with a second graph material, double-sided meshes, a mid-play hole cut, effects, decals and the next map's terrain)
created 35 pipelines outside the warm queue after the first game frame on v0.1.9, 9 of them on the frame thread. It
creates **0** now (asserted).
- **Terrain** pipelines belong to the device (`RenderDevice::shared_pipelines`): a second terrain, its `prewarm` and its
  first hole cut compile nothing. `TerrainPlugin` queues a map-tier terrain warm job itself (`TerrainWarm`); hole-cut
  sets are built by the frame warms.
- **Effects:** `VfxRuntimePlugin` builds the effects context (simulation, sort, radix sort, render sets) on a map-tier
  warm worker (`VfxGpu::new_warmed`). A failed build is not retried every frame.
- **Material graphs:** new `RuntimeRenderer::set_level_material_graphs(graphs)`: hand over every graph the level can draw
  before the warm, and the game-frame warm builds their sets.
- **Double-sided** forward sets and the translucent shadow are part of the game-frame warm.
- A quit during the terrain or effects warm stops between compile batches.
- Still compiled on the frame thread (unchanged, ADR-166): a window's first scene the engine does not warm. Call
  `GameFrameWarm::request()` behind the loading screen to cover it.

## A focused or fullscreen window is always presented (ferrite_platform)
DO27 reported a ~10 min "freeze" of a fullscreen window (Hyprland, Wayland session; the log shows the window unfocused the
whole time): the 250 ms redraw-stall heuristic marked the surfaces hidden, the renderer stopped presenting, and the game kept
running behind a frozen screen. Now a window that has input focus **or is fullscreen** and has counted as hidden (occluded or
stalled) for 2 s is presented anyway (one `warn` per episode), and the loop keeps asking for redraws. Stall and visibility log
lines are rate-limited to one per 5 s. Unfocused windowed and minimized windows are throttled as before. The window-created
log line now names the winit backend (`wayland` / `x11`) and the fullscreen state. Not addressed: the frame time creeping
while hidden (needs a profile of a hidden-mode run).

## Quit timing and the game-warm watchdog (ADR-196)
- The runtime renderer is freed on a helper thread while the world clears; the device step waits for it within its 1 s
  timeout. A free stuck in the driver can't hold the quit longer than that. The log names the slowest parts.
- `GameFrameWarm` lets a warm that is still building pipelines finish (`progress_grace` 8 s, cap `max_timeout` 30 s,
  env `FERRITE_GAME_WARM_MAX_S`) instead of abandoning it at `timeout` (15 s) on a loaded machine.
- Known issue: when the renderer's free outlasts the device step (the bounded case) and the process exits while the helper
  thread is still finishing, the NVIDIA driver's teardown can crash the process after the clean exit has completed (seen in
  `clean_exit_renderer_drop`, whose test process now waits for the helper). A normal quit joins the helper in step 6 and is not
  affected.
- Recommended host quit watchdog: **8 s** (worst bounded case about 5.5 s plus the host's own flush).

## Known
- Flaky under load on the gate box (pass alone): `resize_memory`, `device_lost_exit[surface]`.
