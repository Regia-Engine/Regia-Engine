## Fixed since the draft

Four fixes for problems found by the release validation, the feature checks and review. Each has a test.

- **Every normal quit segfaulted on NVIDIA (release blocker, fixed in `80093b3b`).**
  - The `ferrite-telemetry-probe` thread held a `wgpu::Device` clone, and `Telemetry::drop` did not join it. The probe
    dropped the last device reference while `exit()` was already tearing down the NVIDIA driver. 6 of 6 clean exits
    on the 5080 died with SIGSEGV (exit 139), wrote a crash bundle, and the next launch said "The game closed
    unexpectedly".
  - Now `Telemetry::drop` joins the probe (bounded at 1 s), and the material-graph workers are stopped and joined too.
    Other GPU-holding threads were audited.
  - Result on the 5080 (fresh launches): 5/5 quits exit 0 with no bundle, and 2/2 quits during the boot warm. The
    hardware sweep repeated 5/5 on the 5080 and 5/5 on the 3080 Ti (windowed), and 5/5 headless `clean_exit` runs on the
    GTX 1080. The new `clean_exit` test fails if the telemetry `Drop` goes back to flag-only.
- **`RenderSettings` did not exist during `Startup` on a first launch (fixed in `80093b3b`).** A host `Startup`
  system taking `ResMut<RenderSettings>` panicked when no preset was saved. `RenderSettings` now exists from
  `SettingsPlugin` build. Checked on the sweep machines: `render_settings_present=true` on first launch.
- **A `kill -SEGV` took 5 s to die (fixed in `8faa7633`, `dd40b291`).** For a signal sent with `kill`, the re-raise
  returned into Rust's own handler, so the process lived until the 5 s watchdog's SIGKILL. A sent SIGSEGV now resets to
  the default action and is re-sent. Test: dies after 384 ms with status 139 and a bundle with a minidump. Real
  machines: 606 ms (5080) and 754 ms (3080 Ti). The bundle's fault address for a sent signal is now `0x0`, not the
  sender's pid and uid. Real faults and the panic-abort case are unchanged.
- **The pipeline-cache shader hash missed most shaders (fixed in `8faa7633`).** `engine_shader_hash` covered only the
  core shader library, so a cache built before the hull-look and depth-shade changes still matched. It now covers every
  engine WGSL source (about 90 files in seven crates) plus Rust files that carry WGSL text, baked by `build.rs`.
  **This invalidates every existing local and shipped pipeline cache once. Regenerate DO27's shipped caches with the
  v0.1.0 binary** (§ 2.6). A stale cache only costs a recompile behind the splash; it never draws wrong pixels.
  Remaining gap: a value injected from a Rust file with no WGSL text is not hashed. It costs only driver cache misses.

## 1. Known issues

### 1.1 The three red rows in the release validation

The validation reports **Overall: FAIL** (8 PASS, 4 FAIL, 1 N-A). All four FAIL rows come from three causes. None
of them is a crash, a correctness bug, or a mid-play hitch. The lead accepted all three for the alpha.

**Bench FAIL (bench-5080, bench-5090): the comparison baselines predate intended look changes.**

- `bench-5080` compares against `docs/evidence/perf/do27-perf-20261009/summary-5080.csv`. That file was measured
  on the 10-08 engine (`b059fd2`). The GPU frame is 26–32 % higher at p50 across all 8 map × tier × size rows. For
  example, Switchbacks High 1440p went from 6.12 to 7.95 ms.
- `rc1-perf` bisected that gap on the same RTX 5080 (Switchbacks flyover, 1440p, frozen content). The total is about
  +2.1 ms. About 1.5–1.6 ms of it is intended new quality:
  - GI Depth as the Desktop High/Ultra default (the owner's choice): +1.1 ms, made up of 0.81 ms of probe passes and
    0.25 ms in forward.
  - Contact shadows on by default: +0.38 ms.
  - Reflection occlusion: +0.04 ms on High, +0.13 ms on Ultra.
- The remaining ~0.45–0.55 ms is under-claimed terrain cost, nearly all in the terrain colour pass:
  - `terrain-gi-fix`: +0.24–0.31 ms.
  - `mars-shade-fill`: +0.05–0.11 ms.
  - Wave I–J: +0.15 ms.
- The 5080 numbers in the report are from the **clean rerun** (16:37–16:42, with DO27 holding its jobs off the card).
  An earlier 15:31 run was disturbed: DO27's front end had inherited the GPU lock.
- Against the morning rehearsal on the same card (10-10, `e44dd12d`), the clean rerun is faster on every row. At p50
  it is 3–5 % faster on High and 13–17 % faster on Ultra (table in § 3.1).
- `bench-5090` compares against `docs/evidence/perf-sweep/2026-10-09-0351/bench-do27/summary-5090-0351.csv`. Its
  GPU frame rows are +18–25 %.
  - That baseline was taken on a shared card, and it predates the same accepted look changes.
  - Against the 10-10 rehearsal on the same 5090, the GPU frame is within −4 % to +4 %.
- The 5090 **CPU rows** (`cpu render_into - runq`, `wall`) are not engine numbers. Other jobs were running on
  the build server during the run: the clocks dipped to 840 MHz, and render_into p50 reached up to 3× its baseline.
- Use the GPU frame rows to judge the 5090.

**Smoke FAIL (smoke-gpu, row 7 "Terrain"): a darkness heuristic, not a missing tunnel.**

- The row checks that the tunnel-mouth plate shows a dark opening in a lit frame. It needs at least 2 % of pixels
  with luminance under 28 (`xtask/src/smoke/report.rs`).
- The mouth plate measured 1.2 % (`mouth dark 0.012`).
- ADR-169 (depth-shade) lifted the shaded trench toward the path tracer, and this frame has no visible opening. The
  threshold was tuned to the older, darker look.
- The other parts of the row pass: 4 mouths, 9 samples cut, 4 scatter classes placed, 966/1102 rocks drawn.
- The lavapipe smoke passes the same row.
- The hardware sweep saw the same FAIL, with the same numbers (mouth dark 0.012), on the 5080, 3080 Ti and GTX 1080.
  The row is a heuristic false-fail; the tunnel is not missing.
- **v0.1.1** retunes the heuristic.

**Soak FAIL (soak-bore_twelve): p99/p50 = 1.62 against a target of ≤ 1.3. Open-vista terrain shading, not hitches.**

- 30-minute drive at 2560×1440 on the RTX 5080:
  - p50 6.06 ms, p99 9.82 ms, longest frame 12.00 ms.
  - 0 frames over 2× p50, 0 frames over 50 ms.
  - 0 pipelines, bind groups, buffers or textures created after warm-up.
- The p99 frames are open-vista views with fewer rocks (1123 against 1425). The terrain colour pass rises from
  2.84 ms to 6.18 ms in them; every other pass moves by ≤ 0.15 ms.
- GPU p99 is 9.75 ms, still above 100 fps.
- Switchbacks passes the same soak: p99/p50 1.28, longest frame 12.47 ms.
- A terrain shading perf lane is planned for the post-alpha batch. Terrain shading is also the largest cost on Pascal (§ 1.3).
- In the 60 s smoke soak, both maps' Hitches rows report p99/p50 of 1.33 and 1.38. Those rows still PASS, because the
  smoke gates on creates and > 50 ms frames, not on the ratio.

**wincheck: N-A.** The test machine's rustc has no `x86_64-pc-windows-msvc` std. For Windows, see § 1.4.

### 1.2 Look gaps (all scheduled for the post-alpha batch unless noted)

- **Sub-cell probe leak in small shaded recesses (GI Depth).**
  - Cascade-0 probes are 2 m apart. A receiver inside a shadow smaller than a cell takes light from the probe layer
    2 m up, which sees the sunlit ground.
  - Repro: a rock in a slab's 3.8 m shadow reads 2.5× the path tracer. Balanced reads 2.4× in the same spot.
  - The same leak leaves pockets on the heroes: reactor_a52 panel 2.0×, silo mid-dielectric 4.5×.
  - The fix needs denser near probes or a screen-space near field (ADR-169 known issues).
- **SSGI double count under Depth.** SSGI adds its bounce on top of the probes' bounce. Turning SSGI off moves Depth by
  about 3 %. Not changed in v0.1.0.
- **Mars far walls.**
  - Shaded canyon walls beyond ~450 m lie outside the probe cascades and keep the analytic ambient: about 0.6 of the
    path tracer in both modes.
  - Wall-bounce taps end at 270 m.
  - Near walls improved in v0.1.0: 150–300 m from 0.74 to 0.84, 300–600 m from 0.65 to 0.68.
  - Inside canyons the raster's aerial perspective is also too bright: 0.84–2.2× the tracer's air in-scatter. The
    media lighting assumes an open planet.
- **Europa shaded flats under Depth.**
  - These were already over the path tracer, at 1.3–1.6×, from the same sub-cell leak.
  - ADR-169 raised them another 1–9 % (bore_twelve_wide 150–300 m: 1.32 → 1.43). This is the one change that moved
    the wrong way.
  - Europa trench walls improved from 0.44 to 0.53 of the path tracer, but they are still under it.
- **Glass cost when a canopy fills the frame.**
  - The hull-look glass package (screen-space pane reflection plus enclosure ambient) costs +0.12–0.19 ms in that
    view on the 5080, against a 0.10 ms budget.
  - With an X-11 in a close chase, glass plus the near sun tile cost +0.15–0.31 ms at 1440p. In the default 9 m
    chase the cost is within noise.
  - The fused lookup that would cut it changes pixels, so it is deferred to the post-alpha batch.
- **Hero models.**
  - Under Depth, shade on the heroes is now 1.18× the path tracer (median over 51 cells; it was 1.34×). Sunlit areas
    are at 1.00.
  - Two items were not re-measured after hull-look and depth-shade: glowing trim lighting nearby hull and ground, and
    thin cables breaking up.
  - X12 canopy reflection was 4.7× Cycles on 10-09. Hull-look was judged on the X-11 and was not re-measured on the
    X12.
- **Europa canopies read black from above.** This is physically right for DO27's X-11 glass (base colour sRGB about
  0.14, which transmits 1.7 % per pass) under a black sky. For a visible pilot, raise the glass base colour to sRGB
  0.55–0.60.
- **X-11 glass normal maps (content).** The X-11 glass normal maps hold a constant tilted normal of (53, 53, 201),
  which mirrors the ground as a white veil. Bake them flat or drop `normalTexture` from the glass materials. Details
  are in the DO27 inbox and `docs/evidence/parity/2026-10-10/hull-look/`.

### 1.4 Windows

- **Windows is compile-only.** DO27 built Ferrite in a Windows 11 VM on `green-20261010-0201`. Nothing from this
  release has been run on Windows; the release validation's Windows step was N-A (§ 1.1). The io-robust lane ran
  `cargo xwin check` for the platform, asset, framework and core crates on the workstation.
- **Alpha scope is the Linux tarball.** None of the Windows items below block the alpha.
- **No exit hook on Windows.** The hook that joins warm workers at exit (§ 2.20) is Linux and Android only.
- **DX12: wgpu's FXC fallback fails `lab_forward`.**
  - FXC gives two errors: X3511, it cannot unroll a 16-iteration loop, and X3570, a gradient inside a varying loop.
  - Build DX12 with wgpu's `static-dxc` feature (DXC), or run Vulkan.
  - Making the loop FXC-unrollable is listed for after the alpha.
- **Cursor deadlock.**
  - `ferrite_platform`'s `apply_window_changes` runs on a task-pool thread and blocks on winit's cursor setters
    (seen in a minidump from DO27's VM).
  - Workaround: host-side, in DO27's shell (DO27 has one in place).
  - Engine fix: pin the system to the main thread. It is done (lane `window-main-thread`) and waiting for review for a
    v0.1.x patch or the post-alpha batch.
- **Native crash capture is Linux only.** On Windows (and Android), `native_crash_status()` reports `Unsupported`.
  Panic bundles still work.
- **Auto-quality cannot read VRAM on DX12.** GPU-name rows still match there; an unknown discrete GPU falls back to
  Medium.

### 1.5 Host gotchas found during alpha integration

- **A host with its own `tracing` subscriber must add Ferrite's log ring.**
  - `ferrite::run` and `run_with` install the engine logger, which includes the ring layer. `DefaultPlugins` does
    not. A shell that builds its own registry does not get it.
  - Without it, three things come back empty: the feedback bundle's `log_tail.txt`, the native-crash bundle's log
    tail, and the agent's `logs.read`.
  - Add the ring layer next to `json_layer` and `ferrite::frame_profiler()`, **with a filter** such as
    `ferrite::core::log::DEFAULT_FILTER`. Unfiltered, it records every `trace` event of wgpu and naga.
    The path from a host is `ferrite::core::log::ring::RingLayer`.
  - The host checklist now says this (v010-late).
  - A host stderr layer must also use `NonBlockingConsole` (§ 2.19).
- **Do not draw a host sky dome or any huge opaque mesh that encloses the map.**
  - Every opaque mesh casts sun shadows. DO27's 1.9 km sky dome shadowed the whole map, and that was the "dark
    meshes" report.
  - Use the engine sky (`OutdoorParams` and the planet atmosphere) with auto exposure.
  - v0.1.0 now has a per-mesh opt-out, the `NotShadowCaster` marker (§ 2.21). Use it for any huge enclosing mesh.
    Dropping the dome and using the engine sky is still the better fix.
- **Call `ferrite_framework::feedback::run_crash_monitor_if_requested()` as the first line of `main`.**
  - The game re-launches itself as the crash monitor, and this call turns that child process into the monitor.
  - Without the call, native capture stays off (`NoEarlyCall`). A late call would let the monitor start as the full
    game.
- **Render scale and TSR after a preset change: still needed for preset *changes*, no longer needed at startup.**
  - Fixed in v0.1.0 (review fix S1, `6f0b79c7`): a **saved** preset is applied in `SettingsPlugin::build`, before
    host `Startup`. A host that sets `RenderSettings.profile.render_scale` or the upscaler at startup now keeps
    those values on every launch with a saved preset (test `a_saved_preset_applies_before_host_startup_tweaks`).
  - A first launch with no saved preset now also has `RenderSettings` during `Startup` (see "Fixed since the draft").
  - Still true: any later **change** of `graphics.preset` runs `QualityTier::apply`, which re-seeds
    `RenderSettings.profile` from the preset. Such a change can come from the first-launch detection (a table pick,
    since the probe is off, § 2.16), the player picking a preset, or Auto-detect. If the opt-in probe is on, its
    step-down is another.
  - Re-apply DO27's 4K `render_scale` 0.67 and `TemporalUpscaler::Tsr` on a `SettingsChanged` whose
    `graphics.preset` differs.
  - The Medium tier itself sets `render_scale` 0.67 and Balanced lighting.
- **Window focus throttles the frame.**
  - `ferrite_platform` throttles a window it considers hidden: "no redraw for 250ms; throttling until the window is
    visible".
  - DO27's first 10-minute log was invalid for FPS for this reason.
  - Measure with the window visible and focused.
- **Renderers built outside the warm queue compile on the frame path.** If a shell builds a second `FrameRenderer`,
  take it through `PipelineWarmQueue::take_warmed_renderer` / `adopt_warm_pipelines`. Otherwise it compiles its
  pipelines mid-play (seen at tick ~2693 on DO27's old pin).
- **A host that replaces the `RenderDevice`** (DO27's `bc_device_upgrade`) must:
  - recreate the `PipelineWarmQueue`, or the game warm is skipped with a warning;
  - keep the engine's optional features, including `CLEAR_TEXTURE`. Without it, the SSR wake re-allocates its
    targets mid-play.

### 1.6 Other open items

- **GameFrameWarm: abandoned-warm installs are now kept (§ 2.20).** When the 15 s watchdog abandons a warm that is
  already running, the stand-in keeps the GI field, the reflection probes and the VFX layers, and the late renderer's
  terrain shared draw is handed over when the warm returns (unless the host set its own meanwhile). Still install
  everything before `finish()` / `request()`, and treat the watchdog's `warn` as a bug report.
- **Deferred shader variants pop in.**
  - A material spawned mid-play whose variant was not built during the warm is **skipped** for the frames its compile
    takes, about one compile (0.3–1.4 s on a cold driver). Typical cases are a new glass pane or a blended material.
  - The draw then appears. Nothing stalls.
  - Wrap loading screens in `pipeline_warm::blocking_scope()` to wait instead.
- **Material graphs that miss the warm's 2 s graph budget** draw with the standard surface until their graph set is
  built. This is the same behaviour as on the frame path (ADR-167). `FERRITE_WARM_GRAPH_BUDGET_MS` changes the
  budget.
- **A dormant SSSR keeps its memory.**
  - On Desktop High and Ultra, a dormant stochastic-SSR pass keeps its targets. That is about 185 MiB at 1440p and
    about 416 MiB at 4K, plus a 28 / 63 MiB output, and about 83 MiB at DO27's 0.67 scale.
  - Only `LifecycleEvent::LowMemory`, `ferrite_render::signal_memory_pressure()`, `FrameRenderer::trim_memory()` or
    the VRAM-pressure trims (§ 2.18) free them, and only while the pass is dormant.
  - Low, Deck and mobile allocate none.
- **Native crash capture limits.**
  - No minidump under Yama `ptrace_scope` 2–3, or in a sandbox that forbids ptrace. Check this under Steam's
    pressure-vessel runtime. The bundle is still written.
  - Stacks unwind without CFI (context plus stack scan), so deep stacks can lose frames.
  - A crashed game is killed within about 5 s whatever the monitor does.
- **Feedback.**
  - Prompt strings are English only, and the note is a single line.
  - On Android, `user://feedback` is app-internal storage.
  - A caught panic (`catch_unwind`) also writes a crash bundle.
- **Performance overlay.**
  - "Render CPU" is the renderer's encode, submit and present time, not the whole game frame.
  - Window stats accumulate only while the overlay is visible.
- **Auto-quality.**
  - **The confirmation probe is inert, so it ships off** (§ 2.16). On the 5080 it read a GPU busy share of 1.5 % and
    never stepped down. First launch is a table pick only. The preset menu is the correction path.
  - Rows marked `ByClass` or `Guess` are estimates, not measurements (§ 2.10). Unlisted GPUs get a conservative
    fallback, not a measured tier.
- **Shipped pipeline caches** must be built by DO27 with its own binary and content, **with the v0.1.0 binary**: the
  shader hash changed (see "Fixed since the draft"), so every earlier cache is a miss. Ferrite's evidence caches are not
  for shipping. Android ships none.
- **Shipping quit during a warm waits.** In the shipping profile (`panic = "abort"`) a warm job cannot be cancelled
  between compiles, so a quit during a long warm waits for the shutdown (2 s) and the exit hook's join (≤ 5 s). A
  stuck test job measured 4.2 s. Dev and release builds stop after the compile in flight (§ 2.20).
- **Real driver resets are untested.** Device-lost handling was tested with `device.destroy()` on lavapipe and by
  unit tests. A real reset reports `Unknown` through the same path, but it has not been exercised on NVIDIA or AMD
  (§ 2.17).
- **Pak zero-copy is deferred to v0.1.1.** Raw pak entries are still copied on read.
- **RX 7900 XTX: not run in the hardware sweep** (§ 3.6).
- **Upload-stall fix limit.** The keep-alive pins only the allocator's first host-visible block (§ 2.9). A scene that
  needs several such blocks could still free and re-create the later ones. A persistent mapped upload ring would be
  the stronger fix; it is not needed now.
- **Test margin.** `mesh_terrain_light`'s 0° lit case passes on NVIDIA with a 0.9 % margin (0.959 on the 5090).
- **Stale text in the host checklist** (fixed in the v0.1.1 doc patch):
  - § Translucent shadows says "Not shipped. There is no switch." That is wrong. Coloured translucent shadow maps
    ship: `translucent_shadow_cascades` is 4 on Desktop High and Ultra, 2 on Desktop Low and Deck, 1 on Mobile High,
    0 on Mobile Low.
  - § GI mode quotes Depth's cost as +0.50–0.57 ms on the 5090. On the 5080 it is about +1.1 ms at 1440p. The
    `gpu gi` row reads 0.63–0.75 ms at 1440p and 1.12–1.34 ms at 4K.
- **Deferred by the owner until after the alpha:** Android/phone and Steam work.
- **Tooling, not runtime:** the rig RTX 5080 wedged twice (Xid 62) under hero_ref's lab renders. The cause was VRAM
  stacking in the harness, fixed in `e3501815` (one renderer per camera).

## 2. What's new for hosts

Six alpha features, the warm and hitch work, and the look fixes. Setup details and checks are in the
[DO27 host checklist](../../book/user-guide/do27-host-checklist.md). Costs are on the RTX 5080 unless noted.

### 2.1 Performance overlay (ADR-160)

- **What it is:** a top-left runtime-UI panel.
  - Compact shows FPS, frame time, a 5 s frame-time graph, Render CPU against GPU time, the top 3 GPU passes,
    textures against the budget, stutters, creates after warm-up, lighting option, resolution, upscaler and preset.
  - Detailed adds p50/p99/max, GPU memory, RSS and the full per-pass table.
- **Setup:**
  - It is in `DefaultPlugins` (feature `ui`). The engine default is Off.
  - Ship it on for the alpha with `PerformanceOverlay::default_mode(PerformanceOverlayMode::Compact)`.
  - Add the menu row with `perf_overlay::spawn_settings_entry(world, Some(page))`.
  - The player's saved choice (`GameSettings.performance_overlay`, `Some(Off)` included) always wins.
  - No key is bound. The `"PerformanceOverlay"` action exists unbound.
- **Cost:** CPU +0.011–0.015 ms per frame over hidden; GPU ≤ 0.008 ms per draw. Toggling creates 0 pipelines; the UI
  pipeline is built in the boot warm.
- **Agent:** `perf_overlay.get`, `perf_overlay.toggle`.

### 2.2 Send feedback (`ferrite_framework::feedback`)

- **Setup:**
  - Add `FeedbackPlugin::default()`. It is **not** in `DefaultPlugins`.
  - Put `FEEDBACK_MENU_ITEM` ("Send feedback") in the pause or settings menu, and call

### 2.4 Native crash capture (Linux, ADR-158)

- **Setup:** `run_crash_monitor_if_requested()` as `main`'s first line.
- **What it catches:** SIGSEGV, SIGBUS, SIGILL, SIGFPE, SIGABRT and SIGTRAP, including crashes inside the GPU driver.

  release's `dist/<platform>/symbols/`** (`ferrite package` writes it; it is not shipped to players).

### 2.5 Telemetry and the alpha log (ADR-159)

### 2.6 Shipped pipeline caches (ADR-161)

- **Building the caches:**
  - On each test GPU, run each shipped profile once with `FERRITE_PIPELINE_CACHE_DIR=<dir>`.
  - Copy the `pipelines-vulkan-*.fpc` files to `<project>/pipeline-caches/`.
  - Run `ferrite package --platform linux|windows` (or `--pipeline-caches <dir>`).
- **What `ferrite package` does:**
  - Validates each file (header, key, checksum). A broken file fails the package.
  - Stages each file as `content/pipelines/<stem>-<keyhash>.fpc`.
  - Lists the files in `build_info.json`.
  - A cache built from another shader set or wgpu version warns loudly and is recorded as `matches_engine=false`
    (review fix S3).
- **At runtime:**
  - A shipped file is used only on an exact key match (backend, vendor, device, driver, driver version, wgpu, shader
    hash).
  - Otherwise the engine compiles behind the splash as before. A wrong or corrupt cache draws identical pixels.
- **Also in this lane:**
  - The warm builds every variant a loaded material can draw, culled or off-screen included, in parallel.
  - Rarely used variants build in the background after the first game frame.
  - 20 pipeline sites that bypassed the cache now use it.
- **Numbers:** first launch with a matching shipped cache: 0.60–0.63 s on the 5080, 1.05 s on the 3080 Ti,
  1.37–1.40 s on the GTX 1080 (§ 3.3).

### 2.7 GameFrameWarm: the safety rules (ADR-166)

The game-frame warm (boot-fast, then warm-guard) moves the first frame's compiles onto a worker while the branding
or loading screen is up.

- **Windowed runtime:**
  - Insert `BootSplash` before the app runs.
  - Call `splash.finish()` once the first scene exists.
  - At each later level load, call `world.resource_mut::<GameFrameWarm>().request()` once the level is spawned,
    behind a loading screen.
- **Configure first.** Set the GI field, reflection probes and `reserve_instances` before `finish()` / `request()`.
  While the renderer is on the worker, systems see a stand-in. VFX layers and the terrain shared draw are carried
  over; nothing else is.
- **A warm never blanks a drawable game frame.** A splash request that has not started when the splash is gone is
  dropped, and the scene draws unwarmed. Only an explicit `request()` may hold the renderer.
- **Bounded:**
  - A request with no renderable frame (a view plus a mesh) expires after `request_grace`, 10 s.
  - The watchdog `GameFrameWarm::timeout` (15 s; `FERRITE_GAME_WARM_TIMEOUT_S` overrides it) cancels a queued warm or
    abandons a running one. It logs a `warn` with the warm queue's state, and leaves the boot marks
    `game_warm_cancelled` / `game_warm_abandoned`.
- **Panic-safe:** a panicking material-graph build reports an error instead of hanging the warm.
- **Device guard:** the warm is skipped when the queue's device is not the current `RenderDevice`.
- **Shells driving `FrameRenderer` themselves:** use `PipelineWarmQueue::warm_renderer(...)` with `warm_scene`, as in
  the checklist's code block.

### 2.8 Warm speed and zero mid-play creates

### 2.9 NVIDIA upload-stall fix (open kernel module, no Resizable BAR)

- **The bug:** after any scene teardown in a process (map change, return to lobby, settings rebuild),
  `Queue::write_buffer` ran at about 18 MB/s, a 20–30 ms CPU stall every frame.
- **Where it happens:**
  - Seen on the RTX 3080 Ti with the 615.78 open module and a 256 MiB BAR.
  - RTX 5080/5090 with ReBAR and the GTX 1080 on the 580 proprietary module are not affected.
  - Players on older boards without ReBAR (or with ReBAR off) who run the open module would hit it.
- **The fix:** `RenderDevice` keeps a 64 KiB never-mapped upload buffer alive for the device's lifetime.
  - High, high, low in one process: CPU encode is 2.2–2.6 ms in every case (it was 19–22 ms).
  - The fix is harmless on ReBAR cards.
- Bug report text for NVIDIA: `maintainer-scripts/reports/minspec-hw.md` § 8b.

### 2.11 Hull look (ADR-164, ADR-165)

- **Near sun tile:**
  - An orthographic sun view over the first 4 m around the camera, drawn into the local-shadow atlas. It is 2048² on
    Desktop High and 4096² on Ultra (`RenderProfile::near_sun_tile`), and off elsewhere.
  - It removes the cascade saw-tooth ("garbled letters") on hull flanks and canopy frames, and gives one clean shadow
    at the 1.5 m terrain hand-off.
  - With no mesh within 4 m it is skipped, bit-identically.
  - Cost: +0.04–0.18 ms in forward.
  - Agent: `render.set_near_sun_shadow`.
- **Glass:**
  - The nearest transmissive pane traces its reflection in screen space and falls back to the IBL.
  - Indirect light under a pane takes the pane's transmittance (enclosure ambient).
  - Blended and transmissive surfaces get no SSAO.
  - Result: the milky lit cockpit on Mars is gone, and the panes match the path tracer.
- **Cost of both:** +0.15–0.31 ms at 1440p with an X-11 in a close chase (4.5 m); within noise in the default 9 m
  chase. See § 1.2 for the canopy-filling case.

### 2.15 Boot: the 1.5 s splash and `BootBackground` (ADR-173)

- **The splash is gone by 1.5 s.** `BootSplash::max_duration` defaults to 1.5 s from process start, fade included, once
  the host has called `finish()`. `with_max_duration` changes it; `FERRITE_SPLASH_MAX_MS` overrides it (`0` = off).
  - The cap cuts engine holds only (a running `GameFrameWarm`, `min_duration`). It never fades a splash the host has
    not finished, so `finish()` must still be called (DO27 calls it at sim boot).
  - Boot mark `splash_capped`; `BootSplash::was_capped()`.
- **The game-frame warm continues behind the lobby.** Until it is back, the window shows the clear colour and the UI,
  with no 3D scene. Nothing compiles on the frame path (ADR-166 rules stand). On a cold 5080 with no matching shipped
  cache, a 3D lobby backdrop appears about 1.7 s after the splash. Warm launches are unchanged.
  - The overlay-layers boot job now queues before the canonical frame, so the lobby's UI pipelines are built first.
- **`BootBackground`** is the host's signal: a render resource with `splash_done`, `splash_capped`,
  `game_warm_pending/running`, `deferred_pending` and `probe_active`, and the methods `warm_done()`, `probe_done()` and
  `is_idle()`. **Gate "enter match" on `warm_done()`**, or show your loading screen. Agent: `render.pipeline_warm`
  shows `boot_background`.
- **The first visible frame allocates nothing.** The warm ends with one frame of every window view at the window's
  size, on the worker, then a camera cut. The first frame's history starts fresh, as before.
- **Measured (5080, windowed `boot_probe`, 2 runs each):**

  | Launch | Splash gone at, base `5f78e276` | Splash gone at, this build | First frame CPU, base → this build |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | game scene, cold | 3322 / 3351 ms | 1503 / 1508 ms | 68–71 → 21–24 ms |
  | hull scene, cold | 1727 / 1726 ms | 1501 / 1507 ms | 68–69 → 21–22 ms |
  | game or hull, warm | 971–989 / 661–679 ms | unchanged | unchanged |

  `splash_done` is marked on the first tick after the fade, so up to 8 ms past 1.5 s at 60 Hz. 0 pipelines were built

### 2.16 Auto-quality: probe off by default, conservative fallback (ADR-173)

- **The probe is off** (`AutoQuality::confirm = false`; `with_confirm(true)` turns it on). The reworked probe never
  holds or draws under the splash. It runs as an optional warm-queue job on a fresh renderer, drawing the first scene
  offscreen at the window size. It is off because it was **inert on the 5080**: GPU p50 0.25 ms and a busy share of
  1.5 % of a ~17 ms interval, so its busy guard never let it step down. Its readings are untrusted. The causes
  (`wait_idle` pacing at vsync, a suspect `frame_ms`) are listed in ADR-173; they were not fixed or re-measured on a GPU
  before the tag.
- **Why it was reworked at all:** the hardware sweep found the old probe timing a downclocked GPU (RTX 3080 Ti under
  Xvfb: SM clock 210–435 MHz, 2 of 4 fresh launches stepped High → Medium). The new rules are GPU timestamps, a warm-up
  of at least 0.75 s and 30 frames, no step-down below 0.5 median GPU busy share, p95 over budget **and** p50 over 0.8 ×
  budget, never a step-up, and skipped on software rasterisers or when it cannot step down. Those rules were not
  validated on hardware either.
- **First launch is a table pick only.** The preset menu is the correction path.
- **Fallback for a GPU in no table row** (one tier more conservative than the draft):

  | Adapter | Tier |
  |---|---|
  | integrated, virtual or software | Low |
  | unknown discrete, ≥ 15 GB | Medium (was High) |
  | unknown discrete, < 15 GB | Low (≥ 7.6 GB was Medium) |
  | unknown VRAM (DX12 reports none) | Low (was Medium) |
  | unknown Android GPU | MobileHigh |

  Never Ultra, and never High for an unknown or integrated GPU. VRAM floors and limit caps still apply afterwards.
  Named rows are unchanged. Laptop GPUs now match `nvidia_laptop_*` rows first: 5090/5080/4090/4080 Laptop → High,
  other 50/40-series and 3080/3070 Laptop → Medium, 3060-class and entry parts → Low. (§ 2.10's last bullet on unknown

- **`SettingsChanged` contract unchanged:** a first launch's detection sends one. Re-apply 4K 0.67 + TSR (§ 5.4).

### 2.17 Device lost and surface unavailable: crash bundle and exit codes 3 and 4 (ADR-171)

- **Device lost → exit code 3.** One error log, the `DeviceLostHooks` run, a crash bundle is written and the app exits
  with `AppExit::Error(3)`. The next launch shows the "closed unexpectedly" notice. `crash.txt` starts
  `device_lost: …` and `host.json` has `crash_reason: device_lost`.
- **Surface cannot be created or recovered → exit code 4.** Surface creation is retried once. If it still fails, or a
  surface stays `Lost` for 120 frames, the bundle says `surface_unavailable` and the app exits with 4. This includes
  Android resume. Before, the game kept running with no device behind a blank window.
  - Real-hardware check: on the GTX 1080 (580 driver, Xvfb, which it cannot present to), two launches exited with
    code 4 after about 0.52 s, each writing exactly one `surface_unavailable` bundle; the second launch showed the
    notice for the first.
- **Launchers and installers:** treat exit 3 as "GPU lost" and 4 as "no displayable surface".
- **Exit path:** the exit is requested from an ordinary `Render` system on the main thread, and the process leaves by
  the runner's normal `AppExit` path. `Destroyed` at normal shutdown is not a loss (`note_exiting()` on
  `WillTerminate`). A host that calls `device.destroy()` mid-run now exits with code 3.
- **A host that inserts its own `RenderDevice`** must call `ferrite_render::gpu_health::install_device_hooks` on it,
  or loss detection is off. With telemetry, its callbacks feed the same state.
- **Measured:** exit requested 4.3–4.7 ms after the loss in all three test variants (telemetry, render-only, surface).
- **Not tested:** a real driver reset (reports `Unknown`) on NVIDIA or AMD. lavapipe can only produce `Destroyed`.
  The surface-lost 120-frame limit is a heuristic that no test produces. Cosmetic: the surface bundle's `crash.txt` was
  headed "panic:" in the sweep; the gpu-loss review fixes relabel it `surface_unavailable:`.

### 2.18 VRAM pressure trims and the GPU error count (ADR-171)

- **Trims.** A wgpu out-of-memory error, an OS low-memory event, or allocated bytes above 85 % of device-local VRAM
  (only checked when the card has at least 2 GiB device-local) calls the memory-pressure trim, which frees dormant SSSR
  targets at the next frame. Per-source minimum intervals: OS 0 s, OOM 5 s, VRAM probe 30 s.
- **Budget steps only on real OOM.** Two accepted OOM signals within 60 s halve the texture streaming budget (at most
  3 steps, floor 256 MiB, an unbudgeted policy stays unbudgeted; process-wide, never reset in the session). The VRAM
  probe and the OS event only trim. (The first version stepped the budget from the probe and would have cut textures
  on healthy 8 GB cards; the review caught it.) The VRAM probe compares allocated, not reserved, bytes, so it can
  under-report. That is safe for a trim-only signal.
- **`render.problems`** gains `vram_pressure` (trim count and budget steps) and `gpu_errors`.
- **GPU error count.** The perf overlay shows a red "GPU errors: N (see log)" line only when N > 0
  (`perf_overlay.get` has `gpu_errors`). Every feedback bundle's `feedback.json` has `gpu_errors`; native-crash bundles
  have `null`, because the monitor is another process.

### 2.19 IO never blocks the frame (ADR-172)

- **Non-blocking console.** When stderr is not a terminal, `log::init` writes through `NonBlockingConsole`: a bounded
  queue of 1024 lines and a detached `ferrite-log-console` thread. A full queue drops lines and counts them
  (`LogGuard::console_dropped()`); once stderr drains, the writer prints a `[ferrite] N console log line(s) dropped`
  note. Exit and the panic hook wait at most 250 ms for the queue.
  - `ferrite::run_with` covers this. **A host that installs its own stderr layer must use `NonBlockingConsole`
    itself.**
  - It covers `tracing` output only. `eprintln!` and the default panic hook can still block on a stuck stderr. A hard
    kill can lose the last queued lines. Terminals, logcat, `RingLayer` and `json_layer` are unchanged.
- **Debounced, durable settings.** `persist` writes the temp file, calls `sync_all`, renames, then syncs the
  directory. A change is written at once if the last write is at least 0.5 s old; otherwise the newest value is
  written when 0.5 s has passed. A pending value is flushed on `AppExit` and when the world drops. Remaining risk: a
  change made in the 0.5 s before Android or Linux kills a backgrounded process can be lost; the previous file stays
  intact.
- **Background pak verify.** A blake3 check of each content pak's header hash, computed as the writer hashed it.
  - Runs on a detached thread 60 s after mount, capped at 64 MiB/s. Linux: nice 19 and the idle IO class (the IO class
    only matters under BFQ). Windows: background thread mode. Plain reads, so a truncated file gives a report, not a
    SIGBUS. No profiler span.
  - It runs on the first launch after a pak or build change. A verified pak is listed in `cache://pak-verified.txt`
    and skipped later. Off without a record location (Android). All-zero hashes count as unhashed.
  - A mismatch logs an error ("game data file … is corrupt … verify the game files or reinstall the game"), emits a
    `pak_corrupt` telemetry note, is never recorded (the next launch checks again), and does not crash.
  - `FERRITE_PAK_VERIFY=0` turns it off; `=force` re-hashes now. `ferrite doctor --verify-paks <dir>` runs it
    synchronously and fails on a bad pak.
  - Limit: corruption is reported when the check reaches it, not prevented. A pak truncated under the mapping can
    still SIGBUS, and writes a crash bundle.
- **Non-blocking crash context.** The frame thread only flips a flag; the monitor's connect thread does the blocking
  sends. Updates that arrive while it is busy coalesce (newest wins, `context_updates_dropped()` counts the rest). Each
  context message is one atomic `sendmsg` on a seqpacket socket, so a crash cannot tear it. A crash in that window
  carries the previous context.

### 2.20 Quit during a warm, and abandoned-warm installs (ADR-170, ADR-174)

- **Quit during a warm is safe.** The same crash class as the quit segfault (a worker holding a `wgpu::Device` while
  the driver exits) could occur if the player quit during a long cold warm.
  - Dev and release builds: each queue has a cancel flag; warm jobs unwind at the next pipeline creation, so the
    worker stops after the compile in flight, with no panic hook, log spam or bundle.
  - **Shipping builds (`panic = "abort"`) cannot unwind** (an unconditional unwind aborted the process at every quit
    during a warm; the review caught it). The job runs on, `shutdown` waits 2 s, and the exit hook joins what is left
    within 5 s. **Shipping waits up to about 4 s** in the measured case (a quit during a warm, 4.2 s; a plain quit is
    0.15 s); the code bound is 2 s + 5 s.
  - On Linux and Android an `atexit` hook joins the workers and shuts down any queue never shut down (a custom runner
    without `WillTerminate`). It touches no thread-local and logs nothing. Windows has no hook yet.
  - No pipeline-cache save from a shutting-down worker. Agent `render.reference` trace threads are cancelled and
    joined (1 s bound) when the world drops.
- **Abandoned-warm installs.** When the 15 s watchdog abandons a running warm, the stand-in now starts with the map's
  GI field and reflection probes (besides the VFX layers). When the late renderer returns, its terrain shared draw is
  moved to the renderer that took over, unless that renderer has one or the host set it meanwhile (a host `None`
  stands). A new warm drops an older abandoned one. This replaces the draft's "an abandoned warm loses its installs".

### 2.21 `NotShadowCaster`

- A marker component. A mesh with it casts **no sun shadow and no local-light shadow**, and still receives shadows.
  Use it on any huge enclosing mesh (the DO27 sky dome case, § 1.5). Without it, every opaque mesh casts.
- Add it in code. It is not reflect-registered, so scene files and the agent's add-component-by-name cannot set it.
- No new public field: `MeshRenderer`'s struct literals still compile. The default path is identical by construction.
- Integration test: a marked sphere leaves the floor brighter than an unmarked one.

### 2.22 Scatter spawn stall on 12-thread CPUs (ADR-176)

- **The bug:** a one-time 217–279 ms frame at Bore Twelve spawn frame 31 on the 12-thread i7-8700K (RTX 3080 Ti box),
  High/Ultra only, 2 of 2 runs. `ScatterField::update` blocked on a prefetch task queued behind the GPU probes' SDF
  bakes on the async pool (`cpus / 4` threads, 3 here).
- **The fix:** a needed tile is taken from its task only if the task has finished; otherwise it is placed on the
  calling thread (placement is a pure function of the tile key). Frames and goldens are identical.
- **Measured on the 5080 pinned to 12 threads:** worst frame 55.8 / 193.6 / 198.2 ms before, 3.75 / 3.72 ms after
  (3 and 2 runs). The new test fails with the old `block_on`.
- **Not re-run on the 3080 Ti box.** The repro matches its signature, but smoke's Hitches row there was not repeated
  on the fixed tree. On a starved pool the frame thread places up to the tile budget itself.

**From `green-20261009-1537` (`691d2177`, DO27's 10-09 pin) or `green-20261010-0201` (`e44dd12d`).**

### 5.1 No content changes

- Every on-disk format version and importer version is unchanged since `691d2177`:
  - scene 8, material 3, terrain layers 3, scatter 2, matgraph 2, matinst 2, VFX 4, glasscrack 1, pak 1;
  - glTF importer 1.6.0, `.material.ron` importer 1.2.0, PNG/JPEG importer 1.1.0.
- No recook is required for v0.1.0.
- `user://settings.ron` from an earlier pin loads. The new fields default, and a non-default preset in an old file
  counts as the player's choice.

### 5.2 Only if you are on `green-20261009-1537` (before boot-fast)

- **Boot:**
  - Insert `BootSplash` and call `finish()` once the first scene exists.
  - Call `GameFrameWarm::request()` at later level loads.
  - Without this, the first game frame compiles everything: 5–8 s cold on a 5080.
  - The persistent pipeline cache is on by default (`cache://pipelines`).
- **GI Depth is the Desktop High/Ultra default.** `gi_look: None` means Depth there. Call
  `TerrainSharedDraw::bake_gi_albedo_map` at load. To keep Balanced, set `LightingMode::Balanced` or
  `RenderSettings::gi_look`.
- **`OutdoorParams` has a new field, `planet_surface: Option<bool>`.** Add `planet_surface: None` (or
  `..Default::default()`) to struct literals.

### 5.3 From either pin

- **Add to `main`:** `ferrite_framework::feedback::run_crash_monitor_if_requested();` as the first line.
- **Add plugins and menu entries:**
  - `FeedbackPlugin::default()` (not in `DefaultPlugins`).
  - The "Send feedback" menu entry and the input-skip while `Feedback::is_prompt_open()`.
  - The performance-overlay settings row and `PerformanceOverlay::default_mode(PerformanceOverlayMode::Compact)`.
- **Already in `DefaultPlugins`, no call needed:** `TelemetryPlugin`, `AutoQualityPlugin` (active with
  `SettingsPlugin`) and `PerfOverlayPlugin` (feature `ui`).
- **Subscriber:** if you build your own, add `json_layer` with `RECOMMENDED_FILTER`, `ferrite::frame_profiler()`
  and `ferrite::core::log::ring::RingLayer`.
- **Settings API (source-breaking):**
  - `GraphicsPreset` gains `Deck`. Exhaustive `match`es need an arm.
  - `GraphicsSettings` gains `preset_source` and `detected`.
  - `GameSettings` gains `performance_overlay`.
  - Struct literals need the new fields or `..Default::default()`.
- **Settings behaviour:**
  - `SettingsPlugin` now applies `graphics.preset` to `RenderSettings`. It applies the saved preset at build, before
    your `Startup`, and applies any later change of the preset.
  - Re-apply render scale and upscaler after a preset change (§ 1.5).
  - `GraphicsPreset::Clean` renders as High.
- **Release builds no longer panic on wgpu validation errors.** They log `ferrite::gpu_error` and continue. If your
  shell relied on the panic to stop a broken session, read the telemetry event instead.
- **Warm rules (ADR-166):**
  - A `finish()` whose splash has faded before a renderable frame no longer holds the renderer later.
  - Call `GameFrameWarm::request()` explicitly behind a loading screen.
  - If you replace the `RenderDevice`, recreate the `PipelineWarmQueue` and keep `CLEAR_TEXTURE`.
- **Mid-play materials:** a variant not built in the warm is skipped until built (§ 1.6). Use

- **Packaging:** build per-GPU caches and pass `--pipeline-caches` (§ 2.6). Archive `dist/<platform>/symbols/` per

- **Memory:** budget about 185 MiB (1440p) or about 416 MiB (4K) for dormant SSR on High and Ultra. Forward

  - `FERRITE_GAME_WARM_TIMEOUT_S`, `FERRITE_WARM_GRAPH_BUDGET_MS`, `FERRITE_MATGRAPH_WORKERS`, `FERRITE_COMPOSER_CACHE`;

### 5.5 Host notes for this build (DO27)

From the lane notes in `maintainer-scripts/do27-v010-notes.md`, checked against the lane reports.

- **Exit codes.** Treat exit 3 as "GPU device lost" and 4 as "no displayable surface". Both write a crash bundle first
  (§ 2.17). Other exits you may see: 139 (SIGSEGV), 134 (panic or SIGABRT under `panic = "abort"`).
- **Your own stderr layer: use `NonBlockingConsole`.** `ferrite::run_with` installs it; a shell that builds its own
  `tracing` registry with a stderr `fmt` layer must wrap the writer in `ferrite_core`'s `NonBlockingConsole`, or a stuck
  stderr can block the frame (§ 2.19).
- **Your own subscriber: the log ring and `json_layer` need filters.**
  - `json_layer(path, opts)` with `EnvFilter::new(ferrite::telemetry::RECOMMENDED_FILTER)`.
  - `ferrite::core::log::ring::RingLayer` with `ferrite::core::log::DEFAULT_FILTER`, otherwise it records every
    wgpu/naga `trace` event.
  - `ferrite::run` and `run_with` install the logger; `DefaultPlugins` does not.
- **No sky dome.** Use the engine sky (`OutdoorParams`, `SkyBody`, moons) with auto exposure. For any enclosing or huge
  opaque mesh you cannot drop, add `NotShadowCaster` in code (§ 2.21). It removes sun and local-light shadows, and the
  mesh still receives shadows.
- **`BootBackground`.** Call `BootSplash::finish()` as before. Gate "enter match" on
  `BootBackground::warm_done()` or show your own loading screen. A 3D lobby backdrop is hidden until the warm is back,
  about 1.7 s after the splash on a cold 5080 (§ 2.15).
- **`SettingsChanged` and the 4K lever.** A first-launch detection still sends `SettingsChanged`. On every
  `SettingsChanged` whose `graphics.preset` differs, re-apply DO27's 4K `render_scale` 0.67 and
  `TemporalUpscaler::Tsr`. This is still required and the engine does not do it.
- **A custom `RenderDevice`.** Call `ferrite_render::gpu_health::install_device_hooks` on it, or device-lost and OOM
  handling are off. You still need to recreate the `PipelineWarmQueue` and keep `CLEAR_TEXTURE` (§ 1.5).
- **`FERRITE_PAK_VERIFY`.** The background pak check runs 60 s after mount on the first launch after a pak or binary
  change. `FERRITE_PAK_VERIFY=0` turns it off, `=force` re-hashes now. `ferrite doctor --verify-paks <dir>` is the
  synchronous form for installers and support. A mismatch logs an error and a `pak_corrupt` note and does not crash.
- **First launch picks from the table only.** The probe is off (§ 2.16); an unlisted GPU gets the conservative
  fallback. Keep the preset menu reachable, since it is the correction path.
- **Shipped caches.** Regenerate every shipped pipeline cache with the v0.1.0 binary (§ 2.6).
- **Quit during a warm** can take up to about 4 s in a shipping build (§ 2.20). Do not kill the process sooner on your
  side.
