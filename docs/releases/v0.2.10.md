# Regia Engine v0.2.10

Builds on v0.2.9. This release adds these feature sets for game teams:

- **Water**: an FFT ocean (a seeded wind sea on High and Ultra, the same sea on the CPU for queries and floaters on
  every tier); a shore bake with shoaling, breaking and shore foam; one surface query over every water kind; and
  floating bodies driven by buoyancy forces.
- **Rendering**: thin-film iridescence on the standard material (glTF `KHR_materials_iridescence`); studio lighting
  (sharper studio environments, an image-light ground plane, local reflection captures, backgrounds apart from the
  lighting, image-light debug views, a glTF route into the material detail layer); DLSS Super Resolution behind an
  off-by-default `dlss` feature; and stereo XR rendering driven by the fake runtime (no OpenXR binding yet).
- **Procedural generation, the world side**: generated meshes draw and bake, terrain edits apply and restore bit for
  bit, roads get junctions and decals, biomes read painted weights and density maps, tiled generators bake per cell
  and stream baked cells around anchors.
- **Terrain**: a scatter brush that paints mesh instances, stored per tile, drawn through the scatter field, saved
  next to the map.
- **Destruction over the network** (server decides, clients replay to the same hash) and fracture polish: render
  meshes with normals, UVs and tangents, a `.meta` fracture cook and a Fracture panel in the editor.
- **Lua**: mod state in save games with versions and a migrator, Lua hot reload with rollback, and a hardened sandbox
  (budgeted pattern matching, a call watchdog, capped emits).
- **Gameplay data**: cooked curve tables and choosers in the pak, smart-object definitions by asset reference, a
  cross-platform abilities state hash, and abilities, state trees and smart objects that allocate nothing per step in
  steady state.
- **Editor**: audit panels (Problems, Reference Graph, Size Map), a Layers panel with placed-level editing, Create
  placed level, and a Tests panel. Parked layer members now leave the simulation.
- **Accessibility**: a screen-reader OS bridge (AT-SPI, UI Automation, NSAccessibility) behind an off-by-default
  `accesskit` feature.
- **Platform and tooling**: Android budget verdicts, display-locked frame pacing on Android, mip streaming metadata in
  the cook, API stability snapshots in CI, replay roots and an offscreen pipeline warm for headless render-out hosts.

All new features are off, or unchanged for projects that do not use them, except where **Changed** says otherwise
(parked layer members, the highest-surface water query, Lua budgets, curve tables and choosers in the pak, Android
pacing, `regia package` staging, importer recooks).

The text below consolidates the `v0.1.28-*` release fragments of the lanes merged after v0.2.9 and the integration
notes (ADR-2014, ADR-2015, ADR-2016). The lanes: `accesskit-bridge`, `destruction-net-fracture`, `exit-segv-hang`,
`fft-ocean`, `integ-fix031`, `integ-fix031b`, `integ-v031`, `lua-saves-reload`, `mac-pass-v028`, `net-scale` (an
updated measurement only), `platform-depth`, `procgen-world`, `rr-retest-fixes`, `scatter-brush`,
`spec-remainders-32-37-41`, `studio-lighting`, `studio-regression`, `thin-film-iridescence`, `vendor-upscalers`,
`w1-water-finish`, `wc-data-perf`, `windows-pass-v028` and `xr-stereo`. `fix-audit-wave4` merged with no fragment.

Read **Upgrade: host changes since v0.2.9** first if you build a game or a shell against the engine crates.

## New

### Water: FFT ocean (ADR-1969, N19)

Spec: `docs/specs/water-ocean.md`. Book: `book/agents/water-ocean.md`.

- **FFT ocean** (`regia_terrain::water_body::ocean`). An `FftOcean` on a plane body (`WaterBody::ocean`) is a seeded
  wind sea from a JONSWAP or Phillips spectrum, evolved and transformed by a compute pass into three tiling bands.
  High draws 128² maps, Ultra 256²; Low, Deck and mobile dispatch nothing and keep the swell (the body's own, or a
  stand-in fitted to the spectrum). One spectrum per frame.
- **The same sea on the CPU.** `WaterBody::surface` and `raycast`, `water.surface`, `water.raycast` and the floating
  bodies answer the ocean on every tier from a CPU inverse FFT (`OceanFrame`), built once per tick by the first query.
  It is a pure function of the seed and the water clock; the sea loops every 2048 s.
- **Foam from the Jacobian** of the choppy offset, and a **blend into the shoaled swell** near a shore by depth
  (`shore_start`, `shore_end`).
- **Compute pass in the frame graph:** `WaterRenderer::ocean_pass`, `water_ocean_fft` at `after_opaque`, listed by
  `render.graph`; `WaterPlugin` registers it with the first ocean.
- **Agent:** `water.ocean { entity? }`, `water.ocean_check`; `water.set { ocean, ocean_draw }`; `water.state` reports
  `bodies[].ocean`, `ocean_draw` and `last_frame.ocean_*`.

### Water: shore, shoaling, floating bodies (ADR-1828, spec 87)

Spec: `docs/specs/87-water-finish.md`. Book: `book/agents/water.md` ("Shore", "Floating bodies").

- **Shore bake** (`regia_terrain::water_body::shore`). `ShoreField` holds depth and signed distance to the shoreline
  per texel over a plane body (at most 1024 a side, at least 0.25 m), baked from any bed function (`ShoreField::bake`)
  or a `TerrainMap` (`bake_terrain`) by an exact Euclidean distance transform. Put it on the entity as
  `WaterShore { field }`; `WaterPlugin` bakes one for every terrain water plane automatically.
- **Shoaling, breaking and shore foam.** With a field, a plane's swell shoals by linear wave theory over the local
  depth, capped at 0.78 of the depth (gone on land); breaker foam where waves near breaking, shore foam over the
  look's `wet_width` band. High and Ultra draw it (one `Rg32Float` texture array, up to 4 fields per frame); Low binds
  none. Drawn vertices and the CPU query use the same depth (lavapipe parity within 1 mm).
- **One surface query over every water kind** (`water_body::query`): `surface_at`, `raycast_all` and the `WaterQuery`
  system param answer the highest wet surface (level planes, swell planes, ribbons, terrain planes).
- **Floating bodies.** `Floater` (spheres in the body's frame, fluid density, linear and angular drag) and the pure
  `float_forces` (Archimedes per spherical cap, torque about the center of mass, drag against the water's velocity);
  `regia_framework::buoyancy::BuoyancyPlugin` writes each dynamic floater's `ExternalForce` every fixed step before
  the physics step. `FloaterState` keeps the last step's forces.
- **Agent:** `water.floaters { entity? }`; `water.set { shore }`; `water.state` reports each body's `shore` and the
  frame's `shore_fields`; `water.surface` and `water.raycast` add `depth_m`, `shore_m`, `breaking`, `shore_foam`.

### Rendering: thin-film iridescence (ADR-1977)

Book: `agents/thin-film.md`; guide topic `thin-film`.

- `thin_film` in `.material.ron` v4, `LabMaterial::thin_film` (`ThinFilmFactors`: factor, film IOR, thickness range in
  nanometers) and `LabMaterial::thin_film_map` (coverage in R, thickness in G; `pack_thin_film_map`). A film's
  reflection shifts color with the view angle and its thickness.
- The forward pass draws it through a new `THIN_FILM` variant with its own group 3. Materials without a film keep
  their pipelines and material block. `FrameRenderer::thin_film_stats` reports the frame.
- `regia_pt` traces the same film (`PtMaterial::thin_film`) from the same shader module.
- glTF `KHR_materials_iridescence` reads into the cooked material, with both textures as cooked slots and
  dependencies (`GltfMaterialExtensions::iridescence`).
- Agent: `material.thin_film`, `material.set_thin_film`; `material.shading` lists `thin_film`.

### Rendering: studio lighting (ADR-2000, ADR-2016)

Guide pages: `book/agents/studio-environment.md` ("Which draws the sky"), `book/agents/local-capture.md`.

- **Sharper studio environments.** `StudioEnvResolution` (`Standard` 256 × 128, `Studio` 1024 × 512, `Source`,
  `Width(n)`) through `FrameRenderer::set_studio_env_resolution`, capped by `set_studio_env_budget` (default 4096,
  1024 on Android) and the device limit. A larger base keeps small hot sources in mirrors and gains finer roughness
  steps. `FrameRenderer::studio_env_info`, `environment_resolution`; agent `resolution`, `base_width` and `budget` on
  `render.set_studio_environment`; `render.get_studio_environment` reports the effective resolution.
- **Environment authority** (ADR-2016). `regia_render::EnvironmentAuthority` says which is the sky and the image light
  on a frame with both the physical atmosphere (Desktop High, Ultra, or a planet other than Earth) and an installed
  studio environment: `Atmosphere` (default, the v0.2.8 frame) or `Studio` (opt-in, the environment alone, ADR-2000
  (0)). `FrameRenderer::set_environment_authority`, `environment_authority`; agent `authority` on
  `render.set_studio_environment` (applied before the install) and in `render.get_studio_environment`.
- **Image light ground plane.** `EnvGroundPlane` (height, radius, center, albedo, sun bounce) through
  `FrameRenderer::set_environment_ground` and `render.set_environment_ground`: the image light gives way to the plane
  where it hides it, in diffuse and reflections. Sun and local lights are not touched; nothing is drawn.
- **Image light debug views.** `DebugViewMode::ImageLight` and `ImageLightOcclusion`; agent `render.get_debug_view`
  and `render.set_debug_view` for the runtime, `debug_view` on the editor's `viewport.render`.
- **Background apart from the lighting.** `StudioBackground` (`Environment`, `Solid`, `Gradient`, `Image`) through
  `FrameRenderer::set_studio_background` and `render.set_studio_background`. The lighting never reads it.
- **Local reflection captures.** `LocalCapture` component, `LocalCaptureDesc`, `FrameRenderer::place_local_capture`,
  `bake_local_capture`, `request_local_capture_bake`, `bake_pending_local_captures`, `remove_local_capture`,
  `local_captures`; agent `render.place_local_capture`, `render.bake_local_capture`, `render.list_local_captures`,
  `render.remove_local_capture`. HDR, box-projected, no GI probe volume needed.
- **glTF route into the material detail layer.** `REGIA_materials_detail` or `extras.regia_detail` on a glTF material
  sets `HardSurfaceParams::detail` (`docs/formats/gltf-extensions.md`).
- **Studio frame pin test** (`crates/regia_render/tests/studio_frame_pin.rs`, ADR-1966): a sunlit and a sunless studio
  frame keep their sampled pixels on lavapipe within 2 levels of the values measured at v0.2.7 and v0.2.8; the opt-in
  `Studio` authority is pinned at its own values.

### Rendering: DLSS Super Resolution (ADR-2002)

- DLSS Super Resolution through NVIDIA's NGX on Vulkan (Linux and Windows), behind the off-by-default `dlss` feature
  (`regia`, `regia_render`, `regia_dylib`). Build with `DLSS_SDK=/abs/path/to/DLSS` (SDK v310.9.1, never committed)
  and ship `libnvidia-ngx-dlss.so.<version>` / `nvngx_dlss.dll` next to the executable or in `REGIA_DLSS_PATH`.
  Without the SDK the feature builds a stub that reports `not_bundled`.
- New crate `regia_dlss`: the NGX FFI (bindgen at build time only, with its `sdk` feature and `DLSS_SDK`).
- Device creation adds NGX's Vulkan extensions when DLSS is built and its runtime is found (`REGIA_DLSS=0` turns it
  off).
- `FrameRenderer::set_vendor_upscaler` and `vendor_upscaler_report`: a vendor upscaler is tried before the built-in on
  single-sample TAA frames, falling back to the built-in on any refusal or failure.
- `upscaler::vendor::dlss` (`DlssNgx`, `DlssNgxUpscaler`, `DlssStatus`, `dlss_status`, `VendorFrameReport`),
  `UpscaleQuality::nearest`, `VendorRuntimes::current`, `reason::RUNTIME_NOT_INITIALIZED`.
- Agent: `render.set_vendor_upscaler`; `render.get_upscalers` gains `vendor_frame` and, on the DLSS row,
  `binding_built`, `sdk_version`, `code`, `instance_extensions`, `device_extensions`; `render.set_upscaler` selects an
  available vendor.

### XR: stereo rendering (ADR-1918, ADR-1940)

Book: `book/agents/xr.md`. The fake runtime (`NullXr` plus scripted poses) drives all of it; no OpenXR binding.

- **`regia_xr::XrRenderPlugin`**: the XR frame runs in the `Render` schedule, so the eyes draw this frame's scene.
- **Two-pass eyes** (`stereo_mode: two_pass`, default): `FrameRenderer::render_eye` draws the whole frame per eye with
  asymmetric matrices, per-eye targets, temporal history, shadows and post chain. Both eyes cull with one frustum
  around both (`regia_xr::union_cull_matrix`).
- **Multiview** (`stereo_mode: multiview`): one pass over a two-layer target with a reduced forward set (one
  directional light, ambient, material factors); a device without multiview falls back to two passes.
- **Comfort vignette** after the tonemap, per eye, driven by rig motion only. **Hidden-area mask**: the runtime's
  visibility mask fills depth before the prepass and is painted black.
- **Depth and quad layers**: `LayerList` goes to `end_frame` (a projection layer with each eye's pose, image and depth
  information; quad layers from `XrPanels`, head, rig or world locked, with a priority). Panels over the runtime's
  budget are drawn into the eye images.
- **Desktop mirror** (left, right or both eyes, cropped to fill) and an **XR quality profile** (`apply_xr_quality`,
  `XR_DISABLED_EFFECTS`, reported in `xr.get.render.disabled_effects`).
- Agent: `xr.layers`, `xr.set_quad`, `editor.xr_settings`, `editor.xr_simulator`; `xr.get` gains `render`;
  `xr.capture_eye` gains `source` (`eye`, `display`, `mirror`); `xr.drive` gains `lens` and `mono`; `xr.set_option`
  gains `stereo_mode`, `hidden_area`, `depth_layer`.
- Editor: Project Settings → XR edits `config/xr.ron`; the play toolbar shows the XR status. **XR simulator in play**
  (ADR-1940): the `Simulator in play` toggle or `backend: Simulator` runs play in editor with the scripted runtime;
  the `XR simulator` window shows both eyes and moves the head and hands.

### Procedural generation: the world side (specs 64, 65, 66; ADR-2003)

- **Generated meshes on screen** (`regia_procgen::draw`): ribbons, junction patches, markings and bent pieces draw
  with one upload per published output. `ProcMaterialLibrary` holds materials by path; instance-set `material`
  overrides apply. `spawn.spline_mesh { collision: mesh }` builds a static collider.
- **Terrain edits** (`regia_procgen::terrain_edits`): `terrain.flatten_road` records land when a run publishes,
  restore bit for bit on `procgen.clear`, never accumulate over re-bakes, do not mark their own generator stale, and
  survive a failed or cancelled run. `procgen.verify` compares against the ground without the generator's edit. Bakes
  reapply their records on load, late if the terrain arrives after the generator.
- **`road.junctions`** (crossings and T-ends, cut splines, patches that meet the ribbons within 1 mm, warning
  `junction_sharp`) and **`road.decals`** (segments for hosts that draw roads in the terrain shader). Starter graph
  `starter/road_network.pgraph.ron`. Shoulders draw with `shoulder_material`; markings carry their `color`.
- **`procgen.report`** adds `roads: [{ road, length_m, junctions, flatten_rect, flatten_limit_at_m }]` and
  `totals.junctions`. `RunReport::absorb` merges a tiled run's cells.
- **`Regia.toml [procgen] terrain_edits`** is read by the CLI manifest and the editor; `regia new` writes it as a
  commented line.
- **`in.self`** lists the entity's `Shape`, `Spline` and closed-spline polygon; **`in.entities`** scopes
  `exclude_width_m`.
- **Biomes:** `TerrainLayer` rules read painted weights (`TerrainPaint`); `ScatterClass` sources read density maps
  from the new `ProcDensityMaps` resource; `biome.resolve` declares its reach (`max_core_m`, default 4).
- **Tiles:** `procgen.bake` writes tiled generators per cell; `procgen.bake { entity, cells, tier? }` and
  `world::bake_cells` re-bake named cells; `Baked` generators with `tiles.runtime` stream cells from their file around
  anchors with no evaluation (`pgout::PgoutFile`, `cells_loaded` in `procgen.tiles`); road meshes, decals and terrain
  edits are cut per cell; a terrain edit re-runs only the cells it touches (`stale_cells`).
- **Overlay:** `procgen.set_debug { tiles?, regions? }` draws cell grids by state, anchor circles, priorities,
  seam-risk cells and generator bounds in the `procgen` gizmo group.
- **`procgen.detach`** writes generated meshes as `.glb` under `<bake dir>/detached/` and spawns `MeshSource`
  entities; terrain edits stay applied. New answer fields `meshes`, `unsaved_meshes`.
- **Undo in the editor:** each `spline.*` call that changes a spline is one entry (`Edit spline`); a run or clear that
  changes a generator's terrain is one entry (`Generator terrain edit`) and restores bit for bit. Logs:
  `regia_agent::SplineEditLog`, `regia_procgen::terrain_edits::ProcTerrainEditLog` (recorded only while the host keeps
  them). The editor's bake directory follows the open level, and generator meshes and materials load into
  `ProcMeshLibrary` and `ProcMaterialLibrary` from the project.
- **Samples:** `procgen_roads`, `procgen_biomes`, `procgen_open_world` (shared host `procgen_common`), each with an
  agent acceptance test.

### Terrain: painted mesh-instance scatter (ADR-1936)

Spec `docs/specs/82-terrain-editing.md` § 13, format `docs/formats/terrain.md` § 10, page
`book/agents/terrain-scatter-paint.md`.

- **Scatter brush** (`regia_terrain::scatter::painted`): `ScatterBrush` (density per square meter, scale range, slope
  and height filters, `min_distance`, seed, paint or erase, circle or rectangle, hardness, strength), `scatter_stroke`,
  `apply_scatter_stroke`. Density is a target: painting again with the same brush adds nothing. The class's layer
  mask, exclusions and holes rule always apply. Strokes are deterministic.
- **Per-tile storage.** `PaintedScatter` holds instances per class and per 64 m tile, copy on write.
  `TerrainScatterPaint` is the terrain component.
- **Drawn through the scatter field.** `ScatterField::set_painted` draws painted instances with the seeded ones (same
  LODs, fade, shadows, contact, impostors, GPU instancing); only changed tiles are placed again.
  `extract_runtime_scatter` syncs the store; `ScatterPaintSet` gives the brush its classes in a host that owns its
  field.
- **Undo and redo:** `StrokeUndo::Scatter(ScatterStroke)` on `TerrainStrokeLog`, one editor undo step per stroke or
  drag. **Sculpts re-seat painted instances:** `TerrainPlugin` runs `reseat_painted_scatter` after height edits.
- **Saved** as `<map>.scatterpaint.bin` (format `scatter-paint` 1, magic `RSPT`): `PaintedScatter::to_bytes`,
  `from_bytes`, `load_scatter_paint`, `scatter_paint_path`. It ships raw next to the map. `flags` is reserved for
  foliage wind.
- **Agent:** `terrain.scatter_stroke`, `terrain.scatter_paint`, `terrain.save_scatter_paint`,
  `terrain.load_scatter_paint`; guide topic `terrain-scatter-paint`; codes `scatter_paint_no_set`,
  `scatter_paint_unknown_class`, `scatter_paint_bad_brush`, `scatter_paint_too_large`, `scatter_paint_write`,
  `scatter_paint_read`, `scatter_paint_decode`.
- **Editor:** terrain tool `9` is Scatter (`-` `=` density, `,` `.` class, Shift erases). The level's file loads with
  the level and every save writes it; `editor.save_level` replies `terrain_scatter_file`.

### Physics: destruction over the network, fracture polish (ADR-1967)

Guide: `book/agents/destruction-net.md`.

- **Destruction over `regia_net`** (`regia_framework::destruction_net`, feature `net`; `regia/net` turns it on):
  `DestructionNetPlugin`. The server decides; clients replay to the same `structure_hash`. A per-peer reliable stream
  carries instance changes, breaks (mostly in a short form, 8 bytes each on the sample) and hash checks. A late joiner
  starts from a snapshot; a replica that loses step asks for a new one; bursts are paced by
  `DestructionNetConfig::max_ops_per_tick` and snapshots by `snapshot_parts_per_tick`; moving structures' poses follow.
- **`physics.destruction_net`**: mode, phase, `in_step`, hash, config and stream counters; tunes the pacing; makes a
  client resync.
- **Replica primitives** in `regia_physics::destruction`: `DestructionWorld::{snapshot, restore, define_at,
  set_authority, compact_event, expand_event}`, `DestructionSnapshot`, `InstanceSnapshot`, `CompactBreak`,
  `Destruction::{restore, remove_replicated}`.
- **Fracture render meshes**: `FractureChunk::render` (`ChunkRenderMesh`: normals, UVs, MikkTSpace tangents, split at
  seams and hard edges), `fracture_source` with `FractureSource`, `RenderSettings`. `regia fracture` passes the glTF's
  attributes and gains `--interior-uv-scale`.
- **`.meta` fracture cook** (`regia_asset_pipeline::fracture_cook`): `fracture: Some((...))` in a glTF's settings
  cooks `fracture/chunk_<n>` meshes (slot 0 surface, slot 1 cut faces) and `fracture/destructible`, read with
  `DestructibleAsset::{load, from_cooked_file}`; `lods` names the meshes.
- **Fracture panel** (editor): source, settings, worker-thread preview, explode view, Save, Apply to `.meta`;
  `editor.fracture_panel_{get, set, run, save, apply_meta}`.
- `.fracture.ron` is registered in `cargo xtask check-formats` and `regia upgrade-project`.

### Scripting: Lua state in save games, hot reload (ADR-1943)

- **Mod state in save games.** `regia.state` is a mod's plain-data table, saved in every save game (`SaveGame`, the save
  flow, the autosave, the agent's `save.*`) as a section of the file. Numbers, booleans, strings, `vec3`s and tables of
  them; shared tables and cycles survive. Functions, coroutines, entity handles and tables with a metatable are
  refused with `lua_bad_value` and the offender's path. Limits: 1 MiB per mod, 8 MiB in total, 262,144 tables plus
  entries, 4,096 entities per mod. The mod's random numbers and its entities (name and position) are saved too.
- **Versions and a migrator.** `regia.state_version`; `on_migrate(old, from, to)`; `on_save()` runs before the read,
  `on_load(info)` after a load or reload instead of `on_start`. A mod without `on_load` restarts on a load.
- **Hot reload.** `LuaMods::reload`, `lua.reload`, and an opt-in file watch (`LuaModsPlugin::with_watch`, `lua.watch`).
  State carries over; a version change goes through `on_migrate`, or resets the state when there is none. Any failure
  rolls back: the old version keeps running, its world writes are undone, and the answer names the stage and error.
- **Agent:** `lua.reload`, `lua.state`, `lua.watch`; `lua.list` gains `watch_every_frames`, `last_save`,
  `last_restore`, `kept_state`, `emitted_held`, `emitted_dropped` and per mod `reloads`, `failed_reloads`,
  `last_reload`. New code `lua_state`.
- **Save sections.** `regia_framework::save_game::SaveSections`: a system that collects its own save data with the
  whole world in hand, stored as a `SavedResource` (the save format stays at version 1).
- **Format** `luastate` (`format_version` 1), registered with `cargo xtask check-formats`.

### Gameplay data performance (ADR-2012)

- **Cooked curve tables and choosers** (spec 51 § 4.4). `regia cook` validates `.curves.ron`, `.curves.csv` and
  `.chooser.ron` and stores them in the pak as postcard (formats `curves-cooked`, `chooser-cooked`). A table with
  errors fails the cook; warnings do not; an unresolved nested chooser reference or a cycle fails the cook.
  `CurveTable::to_cooked` / `from_cooked`, `ChooserTable::to_cooked` / `from_cooked`, `CurveTables::load_cooked`,
  `Choosers::load_cooked`. A cooked load runs the same validation as text.
- **Asset registry entries for tables**: `kind=curve_table`, `kind=chooser` with `result=<kind>`,
  `kind=smart_object`, and a chooser's dependencies (`data_cook::registry_tags`).
- **`SmartObject::definition`** (spec 39 § 4): an `AssetRef` to a `.smartobject.ron`, persisted in scenes, resolved by
  the new `regia_ai::smart_object_load` (one load per id, reloads a changed file; a failed load keeps the definition in
  use and reports why). `.smartobject.ron` now cooks.
- **`abilities_state_hash`** (spec 53 § 7): counts exact, attributes on a `1e-4` grid (`ATTRIBUTE_QUANTUM`,
  `quantize`).
- **Spec 53 § 10 budgets**: `MAX_GRANTS_PER_BOOK` (32), `SOFT_EFFECTS_PER_ENTITY` (32), `MAX_MODIFIERS_PER_EFFECT`
  (16), `MAX_ATTRIBUTES_PER_ENTITY` (64), `MAX_MESSAGES_PER_STEP` (4,096).
- **Agent:** `smartobject.set_definition { entity, path | id }`, `abilities.state_hash`; `cooked` in `curves.list` and
  `chooser.list`; `definition`, `definition_path`, `definition_cooked`, `definition_error` per object and
  `index_rebuilds` in `smartobject.list`. New reasons `definition_not_found`, `bad_id`, `grant_limit`; new warnings
  `effect_over_budget`, `attributes_over_budget`, `effects_over_soft_limit`, `message_budget`.
- `TagLookup` and `TagQuery::eval_on`; `regia_framework::table_source::read_table` (source text, else the cooked
  payload).

### Accessibility: screen-reader OS bridge (ADR-1998)

Book: `agents/ui-a11y-platform.md`, `user-guide/accessibility.md`.

  feature.

  `snapshot_scene` skips `PlacedMember` entities; a host level saved before this fix may hold members of its placed
  levels as plain records under derived guids: delete the extra copies and re-save, or restore it from before the
  instance was placed. `regia_scene::placed::reattach_streamed` is new; a host that reopens a streamed level itself
  should call it after `open_level`.
- **Packaging:** `regia package` for Android deletes everything in `app/src/main/assets` before staging; put files
  there through the project's `assets/` folder. A `Remote` session file is now checked (address `127.0.0.1:<port>` or
  `attached`, exactly one instance).
- **Curve tables and choosers:** a game that reads a `.curves.ron` or `.chooser.ron` itself and calls `load_text` must
  move to `register_curve_table` / `register_chooser`: a packaged build no longer has the raw file. An app with
  registered tables needs an `AssetServer` in a packaged build (`DefaultPlugins` adds one).
- **Abilities:** `grant_ability` fails with `grant_limit` at 32 grants (a loaded save with more keeps them). A step
  takes at most 4,096 messages of one request kind (`ApplyEffect`, `StartAbility`, `CancelAbility`, `RevokeAbility`,
  `AbilitySignal`); the tail is dropped with a `message_budget` warning. `AbilityStats::entities_skipped` counts
  settled entities with fixed effects too, and `modifier_evaluations` is 0 for them. The effects pass moves the world's
  change tick by two for each entity whose effect clocks moved; change detection is unaffected. `AbilityAssets`'s
  `Debug` output lists effects unordered.
- **Lua mods:** ids starting with `agent.` are refused; `name` and `version` over 256 bytes are refused. More than 256
  handlers on one event fails with `lua_runtime`; all of a mod's handlers share one instruction budget per delivery
  pass. `table.sort` without a comparator costs three instructions per comparison (about 20,000 items per call at the
  default budget). `gmatch` returns a Lua function that asks the host for each match. A value of more than 16 MiB of
  strings fails with `lua_bad_value`. A pattern with more than 200 nested items fails with `pattern too complex`; a
  pattern error caught by `pcall` is a host error object (`tostring` reads it). New constants `MAX_VALUE_BYTES`,
  `MAX_EMITTED_HELD`, `MAX_EMITTED_HELD_BYTES`. A mod that keeps a value in `regia` under `state` or `state_version`
  must rename it.
- **Water:** `water.surface` / `water.raycast` answer the highest wet surface (see **Changed**). A host that drives the
  water renderer itself calls `WaterRenderer::set_shores` after `set_bodies` in the same order, and should register
  `ocean_pass()` on `FrameRenderer` (without it the layer still evolves the maps, unlisted in `render.graph`).
- **Studio:** a project that wants the studio-lighting look on Desktop High or Ultra calls
  `set_environment_authority(EnvironmentAuthority::Studio)` before installing its environment.
- **Android pacing:** to keep an Android game on the old pacing path, insert
  `FramePacingSettings { mode: PacingMode::Off }`. A runner that waits for the display itself calls the new
  `App::note_pacing_wait`.
- **Quit:** the editor's and the headless showcase's quit can take up to 30 s longer when a driver call hangs, then
  ends without exit handlers (ADR-287's budget).
- **Procgen:** `RunReport::absorb` merges node flags with "every cell" semantics.

## Known issues

### Water

- On Low (and with `ocean_draw` off) a floater rides the ocean surface, which only High and Ultra draw; Low's stand-in
  swell has the same height and peak wavelength but not the same crests. Low also draws the swell unshoaled while the
  CPU queries shoal it.
- Foam is instantaneous (no lingering foam). One spectrum per frame; far normals fade band by band into roughness; no
  filtered maps. Whitecaps thin out with distance (queries are not affected).
- A query on an ocean pays one CPU transform per tick while anything asks: about 7 ms on a shared, loaded CPU core (an
  upper bound).
- No wave refraction over the bed, no run-up; floaters do not disturb the water. Buoyancy costs about 6 µs per sphere
  on a 20-wave swell (100 floaters × 8 spheres: 4.9 ms per step without a shore field, 5.4 ms in deep water with one,
  7.4 ms across a beach, on a loaded build host).
- Ocean and shore GPU cost measured on lavapipe for correctness only; RTX and Steam Deck numbers are pending.

### Rendering

- Thin film: a film on a material graph, a transmissive surface, a masked instance, a lightmap receiver or an x-ray
  override draws without it (counted in `ThinFilmStats::unsupported`); its maps use the first UV set (`texCoord` and
  `KHR_texture_transform` not applied); a filmed draw leaves the GPU-driven batches and the visibility buffer; on
  Mobile Low and Mobile High an opaque film draws without the film (a blended film keeps it).
- Studio: the ground plane takes no object shadows. Local captures are in memory only (no scene format stores one) and
  feed the base specular image light only. On Desktop High with probe GI on, a white metal sphere in a studio
  environment shows a red-to-yellow spot where it reflects straight down (cause not found, ADR-2000 (6)); turn
  `gi_probes` off for studio measurements. With probe specular on, the ground plane's reflection change does not show
  where probes cover a surface. Switching to `Studio` after atmosphere frames keeps the mixed maps until the
  environment is set again. The studio frame pins are lavapipe's; on a hardware adapter the test skips the compare.
- DLSS: XeSS is not bound (Intel ships its runtime for Windows only); XeSS and DLSS on DX12 wait for the Windows pass.
  The frame renders at the profile's render scale (DLSS's preferred size is reported in `vendor_frame.render_range`,
  not applied). Pre-exposure is folded into the exposure input; a pre-exposure change may show a short brightness lag.
  With DLSS active the instance is Vulkan-only.
- XR: the OpenXR binding is still blocked on a headset and an ADR for the `openxr` dependency; nothing ran on a
  runtime. Multiview has no shadows or post stack, draws skinned meshes in their rest pose and blended materials
  opaque, and only paints the hidden area. VFX is not drawn in the eyes. The eyes use the authored sun. A panel over
  the layer budget draws without a depth test. During play the `xr.*` methods answer on the edit world: use
  `editor.xr_simulator`.

### Procedural generation and terrain

- A game sets `ProcGenSettings::bake_dir` itself when it opens a level (only the editor and `regia run --level` follow
  the open level). `regia package` ships `<level>.procgen/` as loose files beside the level, not in the pak (spec 62
  § 8), and `editor_only` bakes go with them; Android games have no baked generators yet.
- Deleting a generator leaves its terrain edit in the ground; clear it first. A moved entity or edited file re-runs a
  tiled generator whole; graphs with road nodes, `terrain.flatten_road` or `graph.call` re-run whole on terrain edits.
  `RoadProfile::pieces` is parsed and not placed. `procgen_open_world`'s hitch report and Pixel 6 run need hardware.
- Painted scatter: foliage wind does not reach painted instances; layer masks read procedural weights; editing a tile
  resets its instances' LOD cross-fade state (seeded instances in the same class and tile can pop one LOD); no class
  picker panel; a sculpt does not re-run a brush's filters.

### Destruction

- The plugin spawns `DestructionChunk` entities without a mesh (drawing chunks stays the game's job). Assets are not
  sent over the wire. A snapshot goes out at about 2.9 MB/s by default; on a much slower link a very large world can
  disconnect that client. With the relevance pipeline on, a client's ordinary entity deltas wait while a snapshot goes
  out to it (about 0.4 s for 1 MiB at the defaults). The stream is not filtered by relevance.

### Lua

- A mod with `on_load` that spawns at the top of its chunk spawns again on every load (spawn in `on_start`). A
  component a game added to a mod's entity does not come back from a save. Events fired but not delivered at save time
  are not saved. After a reload that fails at the `state` or `start` stage, what the old version's `on_save` wrote into
  `regia.state` stays there.
- ADR-1957's text still says a delivery pass allows 16 calls; the code allows one call's budget (ADR-2014 (4)).

### Gameplay data

- A loaded 4,096-row chooser with 8 filled columns holds 2.6 MiB against spec 51 § 10's 1 MiB budget (one cell per row
  fits). Loading a large chooser from text takes about 435 ms (4,096 rows, release) under the locked `ron` 0.12.2; the
  `ron` 0.12.3 bump is left to an integration line (ADR-2012).
- An entity with a conditional (`when`) or `Live` stat modifier is recomputed every step. A state-tree task's own
  allocations are not covered by the budget. With `tracing`'s `log` feature (every workspace build has it), the ECS
  executor allocates one `String` per system per schedule run. The quantized hash cannot prove a divergence.
  `.attrs.ron`, `.effect.ron` and `.ability.ron` still load as text.

### Editor, layers and tests

- `SpawnEffect` particle instances of a parked entity keep running, and a parked terrain still draws.
  `Visibility::Hidden` does not hide a mesh in the runtime renderer in general; only `LayerParked` does. In
  play-in-editor, `Absent` members are parked, not despawned. Placed-level editing closes the host instead of dimming
  it. A `Remote` session reads no device logs and sees no crash.

### Accessibility

- No Android adapter; Steam Deck game mode has no screen-reader path. Not yet run against a real screen reader (Orca,
  Narrator / NVDA, VoiceOver). On X11 the window position is not sent to AT-SPI. The Linux adapter's D-Bus thread
  unwraps its event loop's result: under `panic = "abort"` an unexpected bus failure ends the game (one reason the
  feature is off by default).

### Platform, render-out and networking

- Android: the Choreographer thread, the frame-rate hint and the budget numbers have not run on a device yet. A
  frame's GPU time reaches the pacing model only through a blocked swapchain acquire (wgpu 30 has no display timing
  API). The API snapshot scan does not see items a macro invocation expands to.
- Render-out: the first boot of new binaries can take more than 30 s before `agent.hello` answers on a cold driver
  cache (a headless host keeps no pipeline cache unless `REGIA_PIPELINE_CACHE_DIR` names one). A windowed host's first
  render-out still compiles its offscreen configuration on the frame thread. After the offscreen warm, a headless
  host's first job is still slower than the second (11 s against 3 s on lavapipe). Moving-surface motion repeats as
  far as the GPU's arithmetic repeats (exactly on lavapipe; not re-measured on the RTX 5090).
- Net scale (spec 72 § 14's 3 ms, updated measurement): on a free MacBook M5 (4 performance and 6 efficiency cores, 8
  workers) the mean is 1.2 to 1.4 ms and every tick stays under 3 ms (worst 2.3 ms). On a shared Threadripper the
  mean is 2.8 ms when cores are free and 3.85 ms when busy, single ticks 3.4 ms to 7 ms. The Ryzen 7 5700X class
  desktop the spec names has not been measured.
- Exit fix: the `regia_render` test-process fix has run on lavapipe only (the next NVIDIA GPU pass is its first real
  check). `package_e2e` can still exceed the gate's 1200 s limit on a loaded machine (ADR-1907); the shipping build's
  fat LTO link takes 10 minutes or more of one core.
- The determinism matrix's `race` trace is pinned for x86_64-linux only.

### Windows and macOS

- Windows (RTX 5080, ADR-2008): three `nan_guards` cases and the `atmosphere_golden` sky_noon score (0.978 against
  0.980) fail on this GPU/driver as in earlier passes; `gpu_matches_cpu` needs the Sponza download and
  `third_person --test evidence` needs Python; `session_harness` restart tests are timing flaky.
- macOS/Metal (ADR-2010): `regia_render::ssao_blur_tile` (1/255 blue difference at one texel),
  `regia_framework::perf_overlay::cost_stays_within_budget` (0.128 ms GPU against 0.1 ms on the integrated GPU) and
  `regia_test_utils::gpu_matches_cpu` (needs Sponza) fail. `regia doctor` exits 1 on a Mac without the Android
  toolchain.

## Golden changes

- No golden image changed in the Windows pass. The default studio frames are v0.2.8's (`studio_frame_pin` checks the
  v0.2.7 and v0.2.8 values on lavapipe); the opt-in `Studio` authority is pinned at its own values (ADR-2016).
