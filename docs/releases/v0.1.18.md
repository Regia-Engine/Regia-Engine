# v0.1.18 (patch on the alpha line): emissive fits keyed by content, off the frame thread

Lane `v0118-emissive-hitch`, from v0.1.17. Other v0.1.18 lanes add their own sections to this file.

## Emissive light fits: no combat hitch (ADR-225)

**No visible change on a freshly loaded scene, and none on discrete GPUs' looks.** What changes is when the area lights
of a *new* emissive mesh appear after the first frame: a few frames later instead of in the frame it lands.

What DO27 found (v0.1.1 on Ferrite v0.1.16): 60–598 ms frames while shooting hulls, all on the frame thread inside
`render_into` > `encode` > `frame_setup` > `emissive_lights` > `emissive_probes`. DO27's cause, confirmed in the code:
each hull damage step landed the same intact mesh again as a new `Arc<CpuMesh>` (a bake-key bug DO27 is fixing), and
the renderer's emissive cache, keyed by the `Arc` address, fitted the strip lights again and traced their distance
probes again, synchronously. The first emitter of a frame was always traced whatever its size.

Now (`ferrite_render`):
- **Content key.** A fit is cached by a 64-bit hash of what it reads (positions, indices, texture coordinates when the
  material has an emissive map) plus the vertex and index counts and the material's emission signature. The hash runs
  once per new mesh key and is memoized on the key. On the runtime path the renderer holds a `Weak` of each emissive
  mesh (`FrameRenderer::hold_mesh_identities`, called by the runtime), so the address cannot be reused while the memo
  lives; other callers get a per-frame check of the buffers' addresses, lengths and a 32-sample fingerprint.
- **Probes per object.** The same object (`FrameMesh::object_key`, the entity) landing the same content again reuses
  the probes its previous mesh had. Different objects with equal meshes share the fit and trace their own probes, so
  scenes that place one mesh several times look as before.
- **A reused key with other content is fitted again.** v0.1.17 compared only the vertex and index counts, so a mesh
  landing at a freed address with the same counts kept the previous mesh's lights.
- **Background worker.** After the load frame, a fit or probe trace that is not cached runs on one worker thread
  (`ferrite-emissive`). The frame thread hashes the mesh, copies what the fit reads, and gathers the triangles around
  the lights, and starts no new piece once the collection has spent `EMISSIVE_FRAME_BUDGET_MS` (1 ms; one piece
  always starts). Until its lights land, a mesh that replaced another on the same object draws with the old mesh's
  lights (a damage step keeps its strips lit); a mesh new to its object draws without them, as a unit whose probes
  waited did before. The landed lights are bit for bit those the frame thread makes (tested).
- **Unchanged:** the load frame (the first collection with emitters) still fits and traces everything at once, so
  goldens, lab tests and screenshots of a freshly loaded scene are unchanged; key-0 (ephemeral) meshes still fit and
  trace in the frame; `FERRITE_EMISSIVE_SYNC=1` keeps everything on the frame thread (v0.1.17 behavior).
- **Telemetry:** `emissive` in `ferrite::frame_summary`: `fits_done`, `fits_deferred`, `cache_hits`, `probe_hits`,
  `probes_traced`, `budget_holds`, `stand_ins`, `frame_ms`, `frame_ms_max`, `worker_ms`, `pending`, `background`.
- **API:** `FrameRenderer::emissive_fit_stats() -> EmissiveFitStats` (cumulative; `EmissiveFitStats::since` for a
  window), `FrameRenderer::hold_mesh_identities`, `ExtractedFrame::emissive_meshes`,
  `emissive_lights::EMISSIVE_FRAME_BUDGET_MS`. `EmissiveLightReport::refits` now counts mesh keys that took new content
  (most are served by the content cache); `fits_done` counts real fits.
- **Agent:** `render.emissive_lights` adds `probes_traced`, `probes_deferred`, `buried` and `fit_cache` (the counters
  above and `frame_budget_ms`). Book: `book/agents/emissive-lights.md` § Cost,
  `book/user-guide/do27-host-checklist.md` § Emissive fits by content (v0.1.18), `book/user-guide/telemetry.md`.

Tests (lavapipe, synthetic scenes, no DO27 content): `crates/ferrite_render/tests/emissive_hitch.rs` (100 fresh
`Arc`s of one strip on one object: two fits for the scene, probes traced in the first frame only, the same lights
every frame; a 16 384-triangle emissive lamp landing past the load frame: every frame under 1 ms in the collection
until its lights land); unit tests in `emissive_lights` (the same, on the collection directly, both paths; a reused
key with other content; a replacing mesh keeps the old lights until its own land and those equal the frame thread's;
the landing frame against the v0.1.17 path) and `emissive_lights::fit_queue` (the hash and fingerprint);
`ferrite_telemetry` `frame_summary_reports_the_emissive_fit_cache`.

Not verified in this lane: frame times on a real GPU or in DO27's combat (the lead's A/B: `frame_ms_max` under
`emissive` before and after), Android (the worker is a plain `std::thread`, as the other render workers).

Upgrade: no host source change. No host struct gained a field. The shell does not need to call
`hold_mesh_identities` (the runtime does); a shell that builds `FrameScene`s itself may call it with its emissive
`Arc<CpuMesh>`es. No content format change.
