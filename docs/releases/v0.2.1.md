# Regia Engine v0.2.1

Builds on v0.2.0. This is a look and cost release for the showcase line, plus two new libraries:

- **Look rounds 2 and 3 on the showcase**: the atmosphere's sun and sky tables are read and built correctly (the ground
  no longer sits 4x under the sky), cloud shadows follow the column's optical depth, canopy curtains and foliage
  stipple are gone, stream water loses its chrome and kerb, far snow-patch outlines no longer stair-step, the crash cap,
  dust skirt and flash take their colour from the light they get, the entry streak glows and cools like a bolide, and a
  distant blast shakes the camera visibly.
- **The valley mist reaches headless captures** (render-out, screenshots, the showcase film host): before, it was
  never drawn there.
- **Render-out exposure at cuts**: every auto-exposed job cut is metered before it is delivered, so one-frame stills
  are no longer about 1 EV dark; `settle_frames` gives stills temporal history.
- **Terrain forward cost back for hosts without the ground look**: v0.2.0's terrain forward regression
  (9-10 % of the frame on DO27 switchbacks, RTX 5090) is fixed by compiling the ground look out of the shared pass
  while it is off.
- **Mesh spatial queries** (`regia_core::mesh_query`), ported from geometry3Sharp (Boost 1.0), and **gameplay tags**
  (`regia_framework::tags`, `.tags.ron` v1) with their agent methods.
- Docs and tools: the reader/builder cleanroom rule for behavior-only specs from copyleft source (ADR-1441), an editor
  UI ideas memo (ADR-1462), and A/B, toggle-probe and bisect bench scripts (ADR-1442).

All new features are off, or unchanged for hosts that do not use them, except where **Changed** says otherwise (the
atmosphere tables, the brighter time-of-day sun, far contact shadows, crash smoke and dust, far splat reads, the
entry streak, render-out cut exposure and the headless mist).

The text below consolidates the 28 `v0.1.28-*` release fragments in the tree after v0.2.0: the five showcase lanes
carried onto the renamed line (host wiring, track draw, lookdev 2, snow tiling, X6/X7 finish) and their merge, the
nine look-round-2 fragments, the seven look-round-3 and mist fragments, the terrain-forward, bench, mesh-query,
gameplay-tags and UI-ideas fragments, and `v0.1.28-gate-fix-final` (already covered by the v0.2.0 notes, Known
issues). The cleanroom ADR (ADR-1441) shipped without a fragment.

Read **Upgrade: host changes since v0.2.0** first if you build a game or a shell against the engine crates.

## New

### Look and atmosphere

- **Daylight calibration** (ADR-1425, `regia_render::daylight`): `GreyCard::{engine, ies}` (an 18 % grey card on open
  ground under the engine's sun and sky, and under the IES clear-sky model), `ies_clear_sky`, `metered_card` (where
  the auto-exposure meter puts it), `GREY_CARD_ALBEDO`. Agent: `render.grey_card` (guide topic `daylight`, book
  `agents/daylight-calibration.md`). At the showcase's 21° sun the engine's card is within 21 % of the IES value.
- **Layer albedo report** (ADR-1425, `regia_terrain::layer_albedo_report`, `LayerAlbedo`, `MIN_GROUND_ALBEDO`): each
  layer's tint × texture mean, with a warning when it is darker than natural ground or when the tint repeats an albedo
  the texture already has. `TerrainSharedDraw::set_material` logs the warnings; `TerrainSharedDraw::layer_albedo_report`
  and `GroundLookHandle::{set_layer_albedo, layer_albedo}` hold it; `terrain.get_ground_look` returns `layer_albedo`.
- **Cirrus shading mirror** (ADR-1425, `regia_render::cloud_types`): `cirrus_composite`, `CirrusView`,
  `cirrus_phase`, `cirrus_ridge`, `CIRRUS_AIR_SCALE_HEIGHT_M`.
- **Sun colour and lux** (ADR-1447): `regia_render::sun_colour_and_lux(transmittance, sun_illuminance)` returns the
  colour of mean 1 and the lux of the mean channel, so `colour[c] · lux` is the channel's irradiance.
  `transmitted_sun` and `transmitted_sun_at` use it. `LutImage::sample_clamped` samples a table clamped at texel
  centres.
- **Cloud shadow mapping named** (ADR-1443): `CLOUD_SHADOW_FLOOR`, `cloud_shadow_shade` and
  `cloud_shadow_shade_for_depth` give the mapping from a cloud column's optical depth to the shadow multiplier
  (Beer–Lambert, mixed toward 1 by the sky-light floor).
- **Terrain debug views on a running frame** (ADR-1447, book `agents/terrain-debug-views.md`, guide topic
  `terrain_debug`): agent `terrain.get_debug_view` / `terrain.set_debug_view` switch the terrain's 17 shared-pass debug
  views (albedo, sun split, indirect split, occlusion inputs, normal, ...) by number or name. Rust:
  `regia_terrain::TerrainDebugViewHandle`, `TerrainDebugViewControl` (the resource a host inserts), `TerrainDebugView`,
  `TERRAIN_DEBUG_VIEWS`, `TerrainSharedDraw::debug_view_handle`. The showcase host inserts the control. Errors:
  `F0003` without the control, `F0002` for an unknown view.
- **Terrain debug views 90, 91 and 92** (ADR-1428) switch the stochastic anti-tiling off, the height blend off, and
  both, keeping the rest of the shading.

### Terrain and water

- **Normal-map bias tools** (ADR-1428, `regia_terrain::material`): `Rgba8Image::normal_mean_shift` (a tangent-space
  normal map's mean X/Y offset from flat, in byte levels) and `Rgba8Image::recentred_normal` (the map with that mean
  removed, or `None` under `NORMAL_RECENTRE_MIN`, 2 levels). A tiling normal map should report `[0, 0]`.
- **Far-field splat reconstruction** (ADR-1456): `regia_terrain::far_splat` (`far_splat_fade`, `far_splat_axis`,
  `bspline_weights`, `SplatGrid`), the CPU mirror of the shader's cubic B-spline splat reads (see Changed).
- **Far-field layer pick mirror** (ADR-1446): `regia_terrain::far_layers` (`soft_top_two`, `hard_top_two`).
- **Ground-look variant** (ADR-1452): `regia_terrain::gpu::without_ground_look(source)` strips the ground look's marked
  lines from a terrain shader source; `TerrainGpu::shared_pipelines_ground_look()` says whether the built shared
  colour pipelines run the shader with the ground look (`None` before `ensure_shared`).
- **Stream water** (ADR-1450): multi-scale ripples (broad swell and fine capillary layers) on High and Ultra, and a thin
  broken foam line where a ribbon meets the bank.

### Effects

- **Fire light** (ADR-1423, `regia_vfx::fire_light`): `FireLight { base_intensity, color_temperature_k,
  flicker_depth, flicker_hz, seed, lifetime_frames }` with `sample(frame) -> FireLightSample { intensity, color }`, a
  pure function of the integer frame and the seed; `blackbody_linear_rgb(kelvin)`. The `FireLights` resource
  (installed by `VfxPlugin`) feeds live fires into `VfxFrameLights` under the profile's light budget;
  `FireLights::spawn_crash(&CrashFires { .. })` adds a burning wreck and scattered fires from a seed.
- **`vfx.crash` lights the fires it draws** (ADR-1448): `regia_vfx::crash::crash_fire_placements` and
  `FireLights::spawn_crash_sites` give the visible ground fires' positions and seeds and a fire light at each. New
  parameter `fire_lights` (default true) and result field.
- **Sky light for particles** (ADR-1425): `LayerCamera::sky_ambient`, `VfxGpu::{set_sky_light, sky_light_rows}`,
  `quality::{VolumetricPuff, puff_shade_volumetric, SMOKE_HG_G}`.
- **Crash dust class from the ground** (ADR-1444): `GroundClass::from_albedo` picks the dust class from the receiving
  ground's linear albedo.
- **Lit-puff optical depth and sphere-source flash** (ADR-1454): `regia_vfx::quality::{NEIGHBOUR_DEPTH,
  CHROMA_ADAPT, diffuse_transmission, neighbour_transmission, adapt_chroma}` (CPU mirrors of the new lit-puff terms);
  `regia_vfx::particle_light_source_radius`, `FLASH_MIN_RADIUS_M`, `FLASH_RADIUS_PER_SOURCE` (a particle light 400 m
  wide or more carries its fireball as a sphere source); `regia_render::light_source::distance_attenuation_source` and
  the shader function of the same name in `pbr_lighting.wgsl` (windowed inverse square for a sphere source).
- **A physical entry streak** (ADR-1458; the far-plane fit is ADR-1445): a white-hot head, a trail that cools from
  blue-white through orange to dull red by gas temperature and time since ablation (sodium, magnesium and iron line
  tints), a widening glow envelope, a persistent faint train that gusts, and flickering fragments with short tails. It
  is a small bright point at 250 km and grows as it nears. New in `regia_vfx::entry`: `head_temperature_k`,
  `cooled_temperature_k`, `temperature_color`, `AMBIENT_K`, `COOLING_TAU_S`, `RANGE_FIT`. `vfx.entry_state` also
  returns `head.temperature_k`, `ion_tail_temperature_k` and `ember_points`.

### Camera

- **Shock shake** (ADR-1423, `regia_framework::shock_shake`): `ShockShake { energy, speed_m_s, reference_distance_m,
  max_amplitude, duration_s }` with `arrival_delay_s`, `amplitude`, `to_shake`, `arrival_frame`;
  `ShockShake::showcase()`. `ShockShakePlugin` (opt-in, after `CameraPlugin`) runs a `ShockShakeSchedule` on the
  fixed step; the `BlastShock { position, shock }` message schedules a shake to the first camera rig. Agent:
  `vfx.fire_lights`, `vfx.spawn_crash_fires`, `camera.shock_shake`, `camera.schedule_shock_shake` (book
  `agents/fire-light-shock-shake.md`).
- **Visible blast shake: ground waves tilt the camera** (ADR-1457, `regia_framework::ground_shock`): `GroundShock`
  (yield, coupling, P-wave and air speeds, tripod lever, Q, stiffness), `MountShock` (a seeded damped pitch/yaw/roll
  oscillation at 4 to 9 Hz), `ShakePose`, `WaveKind`, `angle_to_pixels`. A 0.35 kt surface blast tilts a camera
  3.3 km away by about 3 px at 4K (seismic wave, 0.6 s) and 5 px (airblast, 9.6 s), and a camera at 300 m by several
  degrees. `ShockShakeSchedule::schedule_ground`, `ground_pending`, `ground_fired`; `CameraDirector::mount_shock`,
  `sample_pose`; `CameraDirector::sample` is public. `camera.schedule_shock_shake` takes `ground` (default true) and
  `yield_kt` and reports `ground`, `seismic_arrival_frame`, `air_arrival_frame`; `camera.shock_shake` takes
  `yield_kt` and reports `ground_pending` and a `ground` prediction.
- **Camera shake in the showcase host** (ADR-1448): `camera.schedule_shock_shake` no longer fails with `F0016` in
  `samples/showcase`; the host installs the schedule and applies the camera director to the shot player's pose each
  frame (`showcase::shake`), as a function of the frame clock. `regia_framework::shots::ShotDriveSet` names the system
  set of the shot player's camera write.

### Offline output and render-out

  `exposure_metered_cuts`. Book `agents/render-out.md`, section "Exposure at a cut".

### Gameplay and core libraries

- **Gameplay tags** (ADR-1463, `regia_framework::tags`): `Tag` (an interned hierarchical name such as
  `Combat.Damage.Fire` with a stable 64-bit FNV-1a id), `TagContainer` (a sorted tag set and reflected component; `has`
  matches a held child, `has_exact` does not; `add`, `remove`, `remove_branch`, `expanded`), `TagQuery` (`Has`,
  `HasExact`, `AllOf`, `AnyOf`, `NoneOf`, in RON and JSON), `TagRegistry` and `TagTable` (the declared tags), and
  `TagPlugin`, which raises a `TagsChanged` message when a container changes. **`.tags.ron` v1** is the declared-tag
  table, with a validator for invalid names, duplicates and undeclared uses (`docs/formats/tags-ron.md`). Agent:
  `tags.list`, `tags.get`, `tags.set`, `tags.query`, `tags.validate` (book `agents/gameplay-tags.md`, guide topic
  `gameplay-tags`).
- **Mesh spatial queries** (ADR-1461, `regia_core::mesh_query`, book page "Mesh queries"): `TriMesh` (validated
  positions and index triples), `TriBvh` (`ray_cast`, `closest_point`, `triangles_in_aabb`, `triangles_in_sphere`,
  `intersects` / `intersecting_pairs` between two trees under an optional transform), `WindingNumberTree` (fast
  generalized winding number, `beta` 2 by default; `winding_number`, `is_inside`) and `winding_number_exact`,
  `SignedDistanceGrid` with `SdfParams` (exact band, fast sweeping, sign from the winding number; trilinear `sample`,
  `gradient`, `sample_with_gradient`), `PointHash` (`within_radius`, `nearest`), `DenseGrid3`, the `triangle`
  primitives and one error type, `MeshQueryError`. Single-threaded; on a 100,000-triangle mesh the tree builds in
  14 ms, a closest point near the surface takes 2.3 µs, a ray cast 0.7 µs, a winding number 2.4 µs. The tree,
  winding-number, distance-grid and point-hash code is ported from geometry3Sharp by Ryan Schmidt (Boost Software
  License 1.0), with attribution in the files, `docs/ported-sources.toml` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. No agent
  method: the module holds no engine state.

## Changed

- **The atmosphere's sun and sky tables are read and built correctly** (ADR-1447). The transmittance and
  multiple-scattering tables are sampled clamped at texel centres (the wrapping sampler blended the overhead sun with
  the texel pointing straight down). The transmittance rows follow the horizon distance `ρ = sqrt(r² − R²)` (the
  ground used to read the table 1.6 km up and drop most of a haze layer from every sun path). The sky, second-order
  and optical-depth marches use quadratic steps on rays that leave the atmosphere. Against a Monte Carlo of the
  engine's own air, the showcase's τ 0.18 air at a 21° sun now gives sun 0.436 vs 0.435, sky irradiance +4 % and sky
  5°–30° within 5 % (before: sun 0.69, low sky up to 2.4×). Hazy low suns are dimmer and redder, overhead suns up to
  2× brighter, hazy horizons dimmer.
- **The time-of-day sun is brighter on lit surfaces** (ADR-1447): `transmitted_sun` / `transmitted_sun_at` return a
  colour of mean 1 (it was normalised to its peak channel with the mean channel's lux). Sunlit surfaces brighten
  (Earth default air: ×1.08 at 70°, ×1.11 at 45°, ×1.22 at 21°, ×1.44 at 10°, ×1.77 at 5°; Mars ≤ ×1.04); shade and
  the sky do not. This applies to meshes, terrain, probes, the IBL's ground half, clouds, the cirrus sheet and the
  path tracer's `effective_sun`. `GreyCard::engine` reads the photometric sun as `lux · luminance(colour)`. On the
  showcase's look-round-2 stills the sky over sunlit ground went from 3.9x to 2.4x the ground (2.0x settled;
  ADR-1451).
- **Cloud shadows** (ADR-1443): the screen-space cloud shadow (used when the cloud transmittance map is off) weights
  the column's optical depth by the weather value, so thin cumulus casts a faint, soft shadow whose edge follows the
  density field. Full-coverage weather casts the shadow it did before.
- **Cirrus sheet** (ADR-1425, `clouds.wgsl`): the sheet hides only the sky's in-scatter from beyond it
  (`exp(−h / 8 km)`), so streaks are brighter than the sky instead of darker; a two-term ice phase function (mean
  asymmetry 0.75); thin curl-warped filaments gathered into bands.
- **Far contact shadows** (ADR-1449): the assumed thickness of screen-space contact shadows is capped at 1 m
  (`contact_shadows::THICKNESS_MAX_M`); only surfaces beyond 25 m change.
- **Layer normal maps are recentred at upload when anti-tiling is on** (ADR-1428): a normal map whose X or Y bytes
  average 2 or more levels away from 128 has that mean subtracted (`TerrainGpu::set_material`). Zero-mean maps, the
  procedural layer sets and materials with `anti_tiling_cell` 0 upload unchanged.
- **Far terrain** (ADR-1446, ADR-1456): past the terrain budget's `far_layers_m` (on in the Cinematic profile) a pixel
  keeps its two strongest layers by subtracting the third strongest weight and renormalising, instead of a hard pick;
  and splat packs are read with a cubic B-spline (four bilinear taps per pack, half a mip level coarser) as the pixel
  footprint's short axis grows from 0.1 to 0.25 pack texels. Nearer ground (a pack texel over ten pixels across) keeps
  the two bilinear taps exactly; the virtual-texture bake is unchanged.
- **The ground look compiles out of the terrain's shared pass while it is off** (ADR-1452). `terrain.wgsl` marks every
  line of the ground look (ADR-1408); the shared pipelines compile the source without those lines unless a ground-look
  row is non-zero, and then the colour pipelines (plain and hole-cut) and the virtual texture use the full source.
  Pixels do not change in either state. DO27 switchbacks on an RTX 5090 (census, medians of 6 runs on two cards):
  `terrain_forward` 2.40 → 2.01 ms, `gpu_frame` 5.45 → 5.05 ms (v0.1.29: 1.95 / 4.94); with the look on, unchanged.
  Turning the look on or off (`TerrainSharedDraw::set_ground_look`, `terrain.set_ground_look`) swaps the colour
  pipelines; with the virtual texture on, a switch rebuilds the texture.
- **Stream water** (ADR-1450, ADR-1451): reflections are calmer and blurred by roughness, and Fresnel falls with
  roughness; the calm reflection normal fades out from 40 m to 150 m so distant water keeps its motion. The wet band
  beside a stream is a soft gradient whose vertical fade starts at 0.35 of the wet height. The shoreline fades by depth
  and by distance to the bank; scattering shifts toward silt brown with depth.
- **Crash smoke, dust and flash** (ADR-1444, ADR-1454): smoke is soot-grey (shader soot albedo and the soot, cap, stem
  and crater-smoke start colours); lit puffs have a noise-eroded rim so overlapping puffs merge, and cap and stem puffs
  are about 25 % larger. Under a sky, lit puffs are shaded as volumes (ADR-1425) and take their colour from optical
  depth: the puffs around a dense puff add `6 × self_shadow²` chords of extinction toward the sun, the diffuse field
  decays as an absorbing medium (`exp(−κτ)`), and the backlit forward glow passes through the same neighbours, so a
  dense cap is a dark sky-lit grey with a warm sun-lit rim. Bright aerosol (snow powder, albedo about 0.7) adapts the
  sun's and the ground's chroma away by `1.5 albedo²`: the dust skirt over snow reads white-grey. Snow crash dust is
  thinner (opacity 0.55 of soil dust) with a `lifted_earth` emitter. The crash flash has a sphere source of the
  fireball radius (the ground under the fireball is bounded at `I / R²` instead of a 1 m knee) and an intensity of
  4.0e5 (`FLASH_INTENSITY_250T`; 1.0e7 in v0.2.0).
- **Local lights with a source radius above 1 m** have a bounded near field (`max(1, s²)` in place of the 1 m knee;
  `pbr_lighting.wgsl`, `lab_forward.wgsl`, `shared_local_lights.wgsl`). Lights with no source, or a source under 1 m,
  are bit-identical (ADR-1454).
- **The entry streak** (ADR-1458) no longer draws one opaque, saturated, uniform bar: its glow is nested soft ribbons
  whose emission never exceeds `peak_radiance`; bloom comes from the post stack. `IonPoint::half_width_m` widens with
  age; `EntryHead::color` comes from the temperature ramp.
- **Render-out cuts are metered** (ADR-1455): with auto exposure on, every `render.out` job cut (frame 0, each `cuts`
  frame, the first settle frame) renders its game frame twice more, undelivered with the clock and particles held,
  then cuts again with `CutExposure::Fade` (histories restart, the reading stays). A render-out job makes auto exposure
  wait for its histogram readbacks while it runs. `camera_cuts` counts a metered cut once. Showcase 4K stills:
  unsettled within 0.07 EV of `settle_frames: 32` (were 1.26–1.89 EV darker on s1/s2, 0.36 EV on s4).
- **A `camera.schedule_shock_shake` call also queues the two ground waves** (ADR-1457); pass `"ground": false` for
  the v0.2.0 behavior. The showcase host applies pitch and yaw as well as roll, and `CameraPlugin` rigs apply and
  remove the same tilt.
- `VfxPlugin` registers `FireLights` and runs `drive_fire_lights` right after the effect lights are gathered
  (ADR-1423); with no fires nothing changes.
- `regia_vfx::crash::drive_crash_sequences` takes its `CrashEvent` and `CameraShakeRequest` writers as one tuple
  parameter (ADR-1451).
- The VFX frame uniform (`FrameGpu`) is 416 bytes (two sky rows, ADR-1425).
- `showcase` depends on `regia_framework` (ADR-1448). Nothing changes for a showcase scene without the new files.

## Fixed

- **The valley mist never drew in a headless capture** (ADR-1453). A headless app (the showcase film host, any
  `RenderPlugin { headless: true }` app) draws only through the agent's offscreen capture, which handed the extracted
  frame's glass, decals and morphs to the renderer but not its `ValleyMist`, so mist of any density left every pixel
  unchanged while `render.get_valley_mist` reported it active. The capture paths (`render.screenshot` and render-out,
  the 16-bit render-out path, the scene-export render) and the showcase's headless sync now pass the extracted mist,
  as the windowed path does. Frames of headless scenes with a `ValleyMist` change; windowed frames do not.
- **v0.2.0's terrain forward regression on hosts without the ground look** (ADR-1452, measured by ADR-1442): v0.2.0
  drew DO27 switchbacks 9-10 % slower than v0.1.29 on the same RTX 5090 because the ground look was compiled into
  every host's terrain shader; one constant-foldable `max` with the look's local wetness tipped the compiler's code for
  the whole shader. See Changed.
- **Ground too dark against the sky** (look round 2, finding 4; ADR-1447): low suns lit the ground too weakly against
  their own sky (0.81× at 21° through hazy air, 0.68× at 10°, 0.42× at 2°); overhead suns above ~76° were up to half as
  bright as their own transmittance; an aerosol turbidity barely dimmed the sun.
- **Hard dark cloud-shadow ellipses** under thin fair-weather cumulus (finding 5; ADR-1443).
- **Canopy curtains and stipple** (finding 3; ADR-1449): false contact shadows under far objects (anything up to 4 %
  of the view depth in front of a ray counted as solid, so twig cards hung curtains of shadow on the ground past every
  crown); motion vectors of masked meshes (a moving alpha-tested, hashed or LOD-dithered mesh wrote its motion over its
  whole quad; a velocity fragment in front of the visible surface is now dropped); one-frame render-out stills were a
  single TAA sample (see `settle_frames`).
- **Water** (finding 7; ADR-1450, ADR-1451): the "liquid chrome" reflections and the grey kerb along the stream edge
  are gone, wet banks are wet just above the waterline, and a distant stream keeps its ripple motion.
- **Crash look** (finding 6; ADR-1444, ADR-1454): the dark-brown discrete column and cap, the cream dust over snow and
  the flat flash wash, then the brown cap, the cream skirt and the clipped flash patch on the near slope.
- **Far snow-patch outlines stair-stepped** from kilometres away (finding 8; ADR-1456): the height blend draws the
  0.5 contour of the splat field sharp, and the bilinear field bends at every pack texel edge. Outlines are now smooth
  curves at that range; patches keep their place and size. ADR-1446's continuous layer pick ships too, but it was not
  the cause: ADR-1451's bisect showed the far outlines unchanged by it, and the B-spline reads of ADR-1456 removed them.
- **The entry streak** was not drawn when its head was beyond the camera's far plane (ADR-1445: the ribbons are pulled
  along their view ray to 90 % of the far plane with their widths scaled; showcase shot 3 was blank), kept no pixel
  line at range, and then drew as a hard-edged white bar (ADR-1458).
- **The blast shake could not be seen** (ADR-1457): a 0.1 m translation of a camera 3.3 km from the blast moved the
  scenery by under 0.1 px; the ground waves now tilt the camera.
- **One-frame stills and the first frames after a render-out cut printed about 1 EV dark** (ADR-1455).
- **A grid of grey cells in nearly uniform snow at 50 to 200 m** (showcase shot 4; ADR-1428): the Poly Haven
  `snow_02` and `snow_03` normal maps are not zero-mean, and the anti-tiling lattice rotated that bias differently in
  every cell. The engine now recentres such maps at upload.
- **The showcase tracked entity was never drawn in a render-out below 60 fps** (ADR-1426): the host pinned the fixed
  step with the engine's one-step cap, so the director ran once per frame and the track played at `fps / 60` of its
  speed. The director now runs every 60 Hz step of a frame's game time (`DIRECTOR_MAX_SUBSTEPS`, 64). Renders at
  30 fps (the `render.out_start` default) now play the track at real speed.
- Showcase look round 1 (ADR-1425): cirrus drawn darker than the sky; snow dust and smoke lit by the sun's colour alone.
- Tests: `visbuffer::tests::double_sided_instances_draw_back_faces_and_cast_like_forward` compares fin pixels within 6
  levels (was 2); ADR-1447's brighter sky moved a few pixels to 3-5 levels, and the back faces were always drawn. The
  95 % bound is unchanged (ADR-1459). The ADR-1448 fire-light test drops exactly one known fire and checks the count
  (ADR-1451). `regia_vfx` `fire_light` and `showcase` `track_draw` run their GPU cases one at a time (the ADR-1439
  device lock; ADR-1440).
- Integration fixes of the look-round-2 merge (ADR-1451): terrain height blending is on again (the ADR-1446 commit had
  left its bisection bypasses in `terrain.wgsl`); rustdoc fixes for `cloud_shadow_shade_for_depth` (a private link)
  and the `regia_framework::shock_shake` module docs (a redundant explicit link); a repeated copy of an ADR-1421 line
  in `docs/decisions.md` removed (ADR-1440).

## Known issues

### Not in this release

- **Milky water (look round 3, ADR-1460) is not in v0.2.1.** Its lane is a first-pass shader edit that was never
  rendered or tested. The stream seen from 100 m or more still reads as a pale grey path, and near water as a pale
  milky sheet with little depth.

### Look and atmosphere

- **The showcase sky is less blue than a physically exact sky in hazy air** (ADR-1455): 10° up, blue/green 1.33 against
  1.46 from an RGB Monte Carlo, and warmer near the horizon. Cause: the isotropic multiple-scattering table under haze,
  partly hidden by the sky march's start-of-segment transmittance. Follow-up: directional multiple scattering plus the
  energy-conserving segment integral; the reference is the ignored test
  `the_showcase_sky_colour_matches_the_monte_carlo_in_every_channel`. It moves every outdoor golden, so it needs an
  owner look check.
- The zenith sky is 14 % above the Monte Carlo (clean and hazy air); elsewhere within 5 % (ADR-1447).
- Under the cirrus weather the frame's sun is 13 % weaker than with the clear preset; whether the light the ice
  scatters forward reaches the ground's diffuse light was not checked (ADR-1447).
- The screen-space cloud shadow (cloud transmittance map off, the default) still multiplies the whole pixel with a
  sky-light floor; enabling the map for Cinematic is a follow-up (ADR-1425).
- The default Earth air (aerosol optical depth 0.005) gives shade at 0.15 of sun; maps set `aerosol_turbidity`
  (ADR-1425).
- The TAA resolve is unchanged: on moving shots its motion-disagreement test still cuts the history of alpha-hashed
  foliage under parallax, so a fine dither stays on canopies that move against their background (ADR-1449).
- The first two frames after a camera cut in a live game still print at the initial EV (the readback latency); only
  render-out meters before delivering (ADR-1455).
- The static-screen cache key holds neither the valley mist nor its clock (ADR-1453). The showcase measured the
  scene's mist density 0.006 as heavy ground haze at 1 km.

### Terrain and water

- The splat data (2 m texels in the showcase) still sets how detailed a far outline can be; the B-spline removes the
  texel-edge steps, not the data's own shapes. Without TAA a far outline is as sharp as the height blend makes it
  (ADR-1456).
- The rest of the gap to v0.1.29 on DO27 switchbacks (`terrain_forward` about 2.01 vs 1.94 ms, SSAO +0.008 ms) came
  with other v0.2.0 changes (the probe-specular branch in `shade`, ADR-1377), not the ground look (ADR-1452). The
  terrain fragment shader sits at an NVIDIA compiler cliff: time any edit to `shade` before merging.
- The first switch of the ground look on, after the terrain's pipelines are built, compiles a shader module and the
  colour pipeline (plus the hole-cut one) on the next frame; a host that sets the look before its prewarm pays nothing
  mid-play (ADR-1452).
- The silt tint of stream water is a constant, not a `WaterLook` field (ADR-1450).
- The showcase's source `snow_02` and `snow_03` normal maps still carry the bias in the files (the engine corrects it);
  the orchestrator may recentre the PNGs (subtract 19 and 29 levels from R and G). `snow_03`'s patchy albedo still
  reads as patches under the height blend: texture content (ADR-1428).

### Effects and camera

- The crash cap is now quite dark against the sky and its rim glow is thin at 3.3 km: owner look check (ADR-1454).
- No true density volume between puffs: the neighbour depth is a per-emitter constant (`self_shadow`); crash dust does
  not sample the terrain albedo under it (its class comes from `GroundClass::from_albedo`), and its flipbook is still
  the cumulus puff sheet (ADR-1425, ADR-1454).
- The ground-shock coupling, Q, lever and stiffness are estimates to tune against footage; the visible shake relies on
  the assumed tripod resonance and the airblast load (ADR-1457).
- The entry streak's head halo is stepped by four glow layers (ADR-1458).
- Under the default light budget only the strongest lights of 30 to 80 fires show; raise `VfxProfile::light_budget`
  for the showcase. Fire intensities have not been judged by eye (ADR-1423).

### Showcase sample

- Contact AO needs the streamed scatter field; `--entity-scatter` ignores `--contact-ao` (ADR-1424).
- The `features_acceptance` default camera pose is below the ground of the orchestrator's scene; use
  `SHOWCASE_CAMERA` (ADR-1440).
- A step of 1/60 s is not a whole number of nanoseconds, so the first frame of a render-out below 60 fps can run one
  step fewer than its rate says; the swapped hardware has no impact pose of its own (ADR-1426).

### Libraries and tests

- Gameplay tags are a set (no stack counts), are not in the editor inspector yet, and interned names live for the
  life of the process (ADR-1463).
- Mesh queries: outside the exact band a swept distance is an upper bound (usually exact); a closest-point query from
  deep inside a closed round mesh visits most of the tree (about 380 µs on a 100,000-triangle sphere); two degenerate
  collinear or parallel coplanar triangles may report a touch they do not have (ADR-1461).
- `resize_memory` failed once on an RTX 5090 (+15 MB of wgpu-internal indirect-draw validation buffers); six later
  runs there ended within +2 MB (ADR-1459). On the integrated head it fails 3 of 3 runs on an RTX 5080 (driver
  615.71.09): +88 to +94 MB, all in the same wgpu-internal buffers. Not yet compared with v0.2.0 on that card (ADR-1467).
- The UI ideas memo is research; no editor behavior changes until a lane implements an item (ADR-1462).
