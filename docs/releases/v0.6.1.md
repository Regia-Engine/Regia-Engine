# Regia Engine v0.6.1

**Regia Engine is now open source under the Mozilla Public License 2.0** (ADR-10225, the owner's decision of
2026-11-20; it replaces the proprietary royalty licence, `LICENSE.md` v1.x). Your game's own code stays yours and can
be closed; changes you ship to Regia's own files are shared under the MPL; a shipped game says where Regia's source is,
and `regia package` writes that notice for you. There is no fee, no royalty and no acceptance step. Templates, samples
and starter content are **MIT-0** (code) and **CC0-1.0** (assets); the documentation and the book are **CC-BY-4.0**.
The licensor is **Sphenisca Regia LLC**, and the source is published at
**https://github.com/Regia-Engine/Regia-Engine**. Outside contributions come in under a **contributor licence
agreement** (`CLA.md`, signed once through a GitHub bot that the owner switches on later). `regia package` adds "Made with Regia Engine" to a game's
credits by default; that is not a licence condition (`--no-engine-credit`). See the book's **Licensing** page,
`TRADEMARKS.md` and **Licence (MPL-2.0)** under Upgrade below.

Builds on v0.6.0. v0.6.1 is a patch release on the v0.6 line: besides the licence change above it carries fixes
only, from the reviews and verifications of v0.6.0 (determinism, foot placement and IK, net security and the net-soak
gate, agent path confinement, editor fonts and notices). No content format, wire version, CLI subcommand, shader
program or fuzz target moved and no new release line starts, so `regia upgrade-project` has no format to migrate from
v0.6.0 (it only refreshes an untouched template `windows_build.rs`, which gained its SPDX line). A game pinned to v0.6.0
reads this note.

Read **Upgrade: host changes since v0.6.0** first if you build a game or a shell against the engine crates: a few
public structs gained fields, and some physics, foot-placement and server behavior changed on purpose.

## Fixed

### Editor

- **The editor theme no longer drops fonts a host installed** (ADR-8888, amends ADR-8589). The theme's first
  `theme::apply` replaced the whole egui font table with the editor's own, even when the theme set no fonts, so a
  host's custom family vanished and a widget using it panicked. The theme now keeps every face and family of the
  host's table and replaces only the `display` and `prose` families it defines.

### Animation and characters

- **Foot placement** (review findings F1-F6, ADR-8721, ADR-8801): a near-vertical or downward ground normal no longer
  throws a releasing foot; a foot faster than 1.5 times `lock_max_speed` ends its stance at 30, 60 and 144 Hz alike,
  and a drift past `lock_max_drift` re-pins only for a jump of the rig or a slow foot; a platform that carries the body
  carries the planted foot; a probe that starts inside solid geometry (a buried foot, a thick slab) no longer plants
  the foot on the probe origin or the volume's underside; a faded-out planter reports no foot planted; a foot no longer
  sinks into the next stair tread or the one being left, and stepping down it no longer floats at the upper tread's
  height for the whole release (it leaves it 5 cm from the lift point).
- **IK caches** (ADR-8735, ADR-8803). More than 64 `ChainIK`/`BodyAim` (or `TwoBoneIK`/`LookAt`) nodes, or two IK
  graphs evaluated in turn on one rig, re-resolved every chain every frame and allocated on warm frames. A `ChainIK`
  node whose skeleton was edited in place (a joint reparented, a tip gaining a parent) kept its old joint list or cached
  failure and left the pose at the bind; it now resolves the chain again on the next evaluation.
- **Retargeted aims and hinges** (ADR-8735). A retargeted `BodyAim` aimed the target joint's own axis, missing by the
  bone-roll difference, and a retargeted hinge limit could bend sideways.
- **CCD cost** (ADR-8735). A CCD solve did O(iterations x joints^2) transform compositions (about a million for a
  64-joint chain at the 256-iteration cap); a sweep is now O(joints). Poses differ only by float rounding.

### Gameplay systems and determinism

- **Grid wall windows** (ADR-8703, ADR-8804). `regia_nav::det::walls::segments_from_grid_window` panicked in debug
  (and returned nothing or hit a `clamp` assertion in release) for a center or radius far off the map, or on a canvas
  side of `i32::MAX` cells (which `GridSettings::validate` allows); a very large radius looped for hours. The window is
  now clamped in `i64`; output for every window that worked before is unchanged.
- **LP avoidance** (`OrcaSolver::Lp`, ADR-8703). A zero-length segment on the body center pushed the body along +X at
  the speed cap; it now gives no plane. A corridor with about 1 mm of clearance a side made the fallback reverse the
  body or enter a wall at 322 mm/s (see **Changed**). The internal rounding division no longer panics in debug for
  `i128::MIN / 1`. All were deterministic (no desync); Stage A stays the default and bit for bit.
- **Desync diagnosis reads every row at a tick** (finding Desync C, ADR-8724). A second row at one tick was never
  compared and a duplicate left row sorted unstably, so a split in it went unnamed and could count as agreement
  (`diverged_systems`, `diagnose`, `compare_typed`, `diagnose_desync_rows`, `sim.desync_rows`). No hash, word, pin
  or golden changes.

### Networking and Steam

- **Pre-proof answer cap** (spec 120 § 11.4, ADR-8718, ADR-8764). A valid cookie refunded its prefix and the
  server-wide count every time it came back, and an encrypting server answered a plain `ChallengeResponse` with an
  uncapped `Deny`, so one readable cookie funded more answers than `pre_proof_answers_per_s`. A refund now proves one
  exact answer once, and a `Deny` after a verified cookie is capped like any pre-proof answer.
- **Reassembly stall** (spec 120 § 5.2, ADR-8764). An authenticated payload that failed to parse skipped the stall
  clock, so a peer could hold a buffered gap and its share of the reassembly budget indefinitely. Each one now counts
  toward the stall; at the base deadline the peer fails with `reassembly_stalled` and is dropped.
- **Hostile traffic** (ADR-8734). The lobby hub drops a link at its fourth refused player proof (`hub_proof_dropped`)
  instead of verifying every one until the idle timeout. A connected peer's authenticated `Join` whose body is not a
  `JoinToken` scores `MALFORMED_WEIGHT` and is not answered, and resent Joins get one `Accept` per receive. A dedicated
  lockstep host removes a seat that keeps sending undecodable payloads and tells its link. An unvalidated packet
  refused by a stream-window or reassembly-cap check no longer moves the receive clock. A stale never-seen record of an
  unknown kind from a rebind candidate now counts in `rebinds_refused`.
- **Held address slots** (ADR-8883). A held per-address slot (ADR-8733) was spent when the return was admitted,
  before the client confirmed, and also by a ticket presented from another address block. It is spent now only when the
  returning client confirms: a retry after a lost accept is the same peer, also after that peer timed out into
  dormancy (before, the block refused it `full`), and a ticket from another block leaves the hold alone.
- **Shared uplink** (ADR-8883, spec 120 § 1.1). Four connections from one machine, or half the peers on slow downlinks,
  no longer cut a listen server's shared uplink for every other peer.
- **Join drip** (ADR-8883, spec 120 § 5.2). An authenticated `Join`, `Accept` or `Deny` drip can no longer hold a
  reassembly gap past the stall ceiling; the peer fails with `reassembly_stalled`.
- **Hub proof cap** (ADR-8883). A lobby hub now counts a Hello whose player proof verified but whose session was
  refused toward `PROOF_FAILURE_LIMIT`; at the fourth the link is dropped (`hub_proof_dropped`).
- **Net-soak gate** (ADR-8733): `cgnat/10` and `cgnat/32` `reconnect_within_grace` (rejoining clients refused `full`
  after another player of their address took the slot) and `congested/8/listen` `no_wedged_client` (the shared uplink
  cut to 315 kbit/s for eight clients at the first join), two of the v0.6.0 release blockers.

### Tools, pipeline and agent protocol

- **Agent writes stay under the configured project** (security, ADR-8798). With `AgentSettings::project_dir` set and
  no `AssetServer`, `ai.bt_edit`, `ai.statetree_edit`, `anim.montage_edit`, `anim.montage_slot_edit`,
  `audio.loopgen_render` and the other methods under **Changed** wrote or read under the process working directory, so
  a symlink planted in the project could not be checked. `ai.statetree_edit` no longer lets a path through when its
  parent folder cannot be resolved, `level.export_bake` refuses a dangling symlink at the description path, and
  `ai.bt_edit` refuses when the project root cannot be resolved.
- **Agent writes no longer follow a symbolic link planted at the temp name** (security, ADR-10066). The writers above,
  `level.export_bake`'s and the other atomic writers of `regia_agent` (fluid caches, curve and data tables,
  `Regia.toml` plugin edits, trace screenshots, perf-gate baselines) filled their temp file (`x.bt.ron.new`,
  `.x.<pid>.tmp`) with `std::fs::write`, so a link already at that name in a cloned project received the bytes outside
  the project while the call reported success. The temp is now unlinked and created with exclusive create, as
  `audio.loopgen_render` did since ADR-3206; the montage rollback restore uses a fresh temp and a rename too.
- `cargo xtask det-golden stage`: the staged `regia_detsim` bundle keeps its per-target dependency tables (ADR-8498)
  and accepts `ron`; before, staging refused the crate, and a staged bundle did not build on any leg.

### Core, licence and security

- **`FileSink` prunes only the session logs it created**; a foreign `.log` file in the log folder is never deleted.
- **Notices** named no editor font and gave no copyright line or licence text for any font compiled into a binary
  (see **New**).
- **Font copyright lines** (licence, ADR-10066). The notices copied each font's sidecar licence header, which named
  other years or wording than the font files that ship (Geist 2024, Inter 2016, Noto Color Emoji 2022, Noto Sans
  Hebrew 2024, Noto Sans KR and SC `(c) 2014-2021 Adobe ...`; the editor Space Mono file joined four notices on one
  line). The sidecars now match the fonts, and each bullet is the font's own copyright record (OpenType name ID 0).

## Changed

- **Raycasts from inside a shape** (ADR-8721, ADR-8801, ADR-8852). `PhysicsQuery::raycast` / `WorldQueries::raycast`
  from more than 1 mm inside a shape report the first surface crossed, never the origin at distance 0, with the exit
  face's normal pointing out along the ray; `raycast_all` reports the exit. A ray starting on a surface still reports
  it at 0. A nearer hit from the heightfield cell-line rescue (ADR-8715) is an entry.
- **Foot planting** (ADR-8721). `FootPlanter::pin` reports the pin only while the stance holds it; during a release,
  hosts and `FootPlacement` probe under the clip foot, and the lift-off ground is a floor until the release ends.
  `GroundProbes::probe` drops hits at or above the ray origin, back faces and non-finite hits.
- **IK** (ADR-8735, ADR-8803). `ik_retarget::retarget_graph` turns `BodyAim` axes and hinge-limit axes through the
  paired joints' rest frames (a `Uniform` hinge may become `PerJoint`; cone limits are unchanged). IK caches keep a
  slot per live node and replace slots unused for more than 4 evaluations; a warm `ChainIK` checks its chain's parent
  links every evaluation (no lookup, no allocation).
- **`OrcaSolver::Lp`** (ADR-8703). Tied fallback velocities between parallel walls resolve to the one nearest the
  preference, so the body keeps moving along the corridor; walls that conflict only through the 2 mm/s margin stay
  hard without it (still `feasible = false`). `OrcaLpStats::skipped_far` also counts on-center zero-length segments.
  The LP corpus pin `0x6ad94cefd7ba8a30` and the Stage A pin `0xf1a197b3f232f005` are unchanged.
- **Desync diagnosis** (ADR-8724). A system splits at a tick unless all its words there are one value, and such a
  tick is never `last_agreed`. `diagnosis_lines` adds a last `more rows on one side` line when a shared tick held
  surplus rows; with one row a tick the lines are unchanged.
- **Per-address slot holds** (ADR-8733 (1), amends ADR-3283). With `ServerConfig::resume_grace_ms` above 0 (`NetLink`
  keeps 60 s), a player that leaves holding its ticket keeps its `max_peers_per_ip` slot for the grace, and its return
  from the same block is not a new peer. A fresh join may borrow at most one held slot. A live takeover at a full
  address is no longer refused `full`.
- **Listen servers' shared uplink** (ADR-8733 (2), ADR-8883) reads a host queue only when at least four peers that
  delivered data, and a strict majority of the peers, see a queue over 25 ms at once (`cc::UPLINK_SHARED_QUEUE_PEERS`,
  median `cc::host_queue_ms`); the connections of one address block count as one witness
  (`cc::host_queue_ms_by_block`). With one to three
  receiving clients the aggregate no longer cuts on queueing; `server_rate_bytes` still caps. The net-soak `cgnat`
  profile keeps reconnect tickets for 60 s and its rejoins present them.
- `verify_player_proof` checks the binding before the low-order key check: a proof for another link is refused as
  `KeyMismatch` with two X25519 operations instead of four (ADR-8734).
- **Agent project root** (ADR-8798). With `project_dir` set and no `AssetServer`, project-relative paths of the agent
  methods above, `audio.render_check` and `seeing.dump_resource` `out_dir`, `level.export_bake`, and the project
  reads of `logic.*`, `statetree.*` (includes too), `vfx` system assets and `fuzz.run` fixtures resolve under
  `project_dir`. The working directory is the root only when neither root is set (ADR-7871).
- **Notices checks** (ADR-8888). `cargo xtask notices`, `notices --check` and `demo-notices` fail when a shipping
  third-party asset has no `[[notice]]` row or an incomplete one (no matching file, no licence file, no `Copyright`
  line, no OFL text for an OFL-1.1 font, unknown ported source).
  They also fail when a bundled `.ttf`/`.otf` font's own copyright record (name ID 0) is not a copyright line of its
  notice's licence file, and they bullet that record first (ADR-10066). A licence-file header line starting with
  `(c)` or `©` counts as a copyright line.

  exits non-zero on any hit. The v0.6.1 public repository is that snapshot.
- **`cargo xtask spdx [--check | --write]`** (ADR-10225): every first-party `.rs`/`.wgsl` file starts with
  `// SPDX-License-Identifier: MPL-2.0` (`MIT-0` in samples and templates; a ported file's upstream id). Preflight and

- **Editor fonts** (ADR-8888). `regia_editor::theme::set_host_fonts(ctx, defs)` gives the theme the host's font table

## Upgrade: host changes since v0.6.0

### Struct fields, methods and signatures

- `regia_detsim::hash::DivergenceReport` (and `regia_framework::desync_hash::DivergenceReport`) has a new public field
  `unmatched: Vec<UnmatchedRow>`: struct literals must add it (`unmatched: Vec::new()`).
- `regia_net::EndpointTotals` has a new field `held_slot_returns` (`#[serde(default)]`): struct literals need it (or
  `..Default::default()`).
- `regia_net::LockstepHostGame` has a new public field `seats_removed` (it already had private fields, so no literal
  breaks). `LockstepSeatLink` gains a provided method `refuse(seat, code)` (default no-op): implement it to drop a
  removed seat's connection (a `NetServer` transport: `kick(peer, DisconnectReason::MalformedPacket)`). Games that call
  `lockstep::net::host_receive` themselves should feed refusals to a `SeatScores` and kick the peer when `charge`
  returns true.
- New public items: `Retargeter::retarget_joint_direction`, `PlantLock::carry`, `FootPlanter::carry`,
  `GroundProbe::probe_moving` (provided), `GroundProbes::probe_moving`, `WorldQueries::point_velocity`,
  `PhysicsQuery::point_velocity`, `regia_net::cc::host_queue_ms`, `cc::UPLINK_SHARED_QUEUE_PEERS`,
  `regia_net_soak::profiles::Profile::server_resume_grace_ms`, `CGNAT_RESUME_GRACE_MS`, and the items under **New**.
- xtask only: `xtask::notices::render_crates` takes the resolved `[[notice]]` rows (`&[ResolvedNotice]`) as a new
  third argument.

### Behavior and defaults

- `raycast` from more than 1 mm inside a shape no longer returns distance 0: "am I inside" tests should use
  `closest_point` (`is_inside`). Code that expected an inside-start hit's normal to point into the shape should negate
  it. Rays that start outside or on a surface are unchanged.
- `FootPlanter::pin` is `None` during a release: probe under the animated foot, and read `FootPlantLegOut::lock` for
  the lock weight. `FootPlantReport::planted` is 0 for every leg while the effect is faded out (it was 1).
- `ik_retarget::retarget_graph` output changes for `BodyAim` axes and hinge limits when the paired joints' rest
  orientations differ: re-run the retarget rather than keeping hand-fixed axes.
- `OrcaSolver::Lp` results change only for parallel or nearly parallel soft walls in the fallback, margin-only wall
  conflicts and an on-center point segment: re-record LP replays through such scenes.
- With `resume_grace_ms` above 0, dormant players and goodbye holds count against `max_peers_per_ip` for fresh joins
  (one held slot may be borrowed): a dormant player's address block is one slot tighter during the grace.
- Hub dashboards that list `refused` codes: add `hub_proof_dropped`.
- `net.status` totals `held_slot_returns` counts a held-slot return when its client confirms the accept, one round
  trip after the join is admitted. `cc::host_queue_ms` reads 0 when the peers split evenly (four of eight is no longer
  enough).
- Headless agent hosts with `project_dir` set find the files of the methods above under `project_dir`, not the working
  directory; a relative `audio.loopgen_render` `out_dir` reply names the project path; `ai.bt_edit` and
  `ai.statetree_edit` under a project root that does not exist answer `bad_path`.

### Editor fonts and notices

- The editor theme now merges over the host's egui font table instead of replacing it. Hosts that install their own
  fonts at start-up (before egui's first pass) or in the same pass as the first `theme::apply` should call
  `regia_editor::theme::set_host_fonts(&ctx, defs)` instead of `ctx.set_fonts(defs)`. Fonts set with `ctx.set_fonts`
  in an earlier pass are kept. A family named `display` or `prose` is replaced by the theme's. Hosts that install no
  fonts get the same table as in v0.6.0.
- `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` gains the "Bundled fonts and assets" section. Redistributors that ship the engine's notices
  file should take the new copy: it adds the font copyright lines and the OFL text that redistributing the
  compiled-in fonts requires.
  The copyright lines are each font's own record (ADR-10066), so they differ from the release-candidate copies.
- A shipping third-party asset now requires a `[[notice]]` row in `docs/asset-licences.toml` (name, licence, files,
  and `licence_file` beside the files or `ported`) besides its `[[asset]]` row; then run `cargo xtask notices`.

### Agent protocol

- `sim.desync_rows`: `report` has a new `unmatched` array (`[{ side, tick, index, tick_shared }]`); clients that reject
  unknown keys must accept it. `lobby.hub_status` has `hub_proof_dropped`; `net.status` totals have
  `held_slot_returns`.
- `editor.about` answers two new keys, `license_spdx` (`"MPL-2.0"`) and `license_url`; `license_title` is the first
  line of `LICENSE` ("Mozilla Public License Version 2.0") and `license_path` names `LICENSE` (ADR-10225).

### Licence (MPL-2.0)

- The engine is MPL-2.0 (ADR-10225). The workspace declares `license = "MPL-2.0"`; `license-file = "LICENSE.md"` and
  `LICENSE.md` are gone. A game workspace that pointed a `license-file` at the engine's `LICENSE.md` must drop it.
- Packages gain `REGIA-ENGINE-NOTICE.txt`, `REGIA-ENGINE-LICENSE.txt` and, by default, `CREDITS.txt` with "Made with
  Regia Engine". A package no longer carries the engine's `LICENSE.md`; a game's own `LICENSE.md` is still copied.
  The notice names https://github.com/Regia-Engine/Regia-Engine by default. Set `[package] engine_source_url` before
  you distribute a game whose engine files you changed (to where you publish them), and replace a `<release host>`
  value left from a v0.6.1 pre-release; turn the credit off with `--no-engine-credit` or
  `[package] engine_credit = false`.
- Engine test bundles (`cargo xtask dist`) and the demo package ship `licenses/LICENSE` instead of
  `licenses/LICENSE.md`, and their `README.txt` / `CREDITS.txt` name the source location.
- The editor's Help ▸ About shows "Mozilla Public License 2.0" with a link to the licence text.
- The project template build script `windows_build.rs` starts with an SPDX line: `regia upgrade-project` replaces an
  untouched copy from v0.6.0 or earlier (its hash moved to the known earlier texts); an edited copy is left alone.
  Template and sample source files carry `// SPDX-License-Identifier: MIT-0`.
- `cargo deny check licenses` now checks the workspace's own crates (`[licenses.private] ignore = false`) and allows
  `MIT-0`.

## Known issues

- `asym-bufferbloat/8` and `/32` `reconnect_within_grace` stay red in the net-soak gate plan by decision: a rejoin is
  the whole three-round-trip handshake, about 3.1-3.4 s over the profile's 1 s downstream queue against the 3,000 ms
  grace. The fix (a one-round-trip ticket resume, wire 6, ADR-8733 (3)) is a v0.7 wire change.
- An authenticated record of an unknown sealed kind still does not run the reassembly stall clock. It is scored, and
  the 20 s silence timeout removes a peer that sends only those (ADR-8883).
- A client whose `KeyInit` or `ChallengeResponse` arrives more than one second boundary after its `Challenge` (a
  round trip above one second) no longer refunds its prefix's token; the answer stays counted (ADR-8764).
- The pending-join path's undecodable `JoinToken` still only counts `malformed` (no peer exists yet to score; the row
  ages out). ADR-8666 residuals (a'), (a'') and (d) are unchanged.
- `cargo xtask perf-budgets --counts-only --enforce` fails two rows, as at v0.6.0: `net_relevance_n214_allocs`
  (38,677 against 37,000) and `_p99` (46,387 against 38,000). This is a v0.6.0 regression, not a platform difference:
  the budget commit measures the same 35,392 / 36,180 on Linux and macOS, and v0.6.0 sends about 2.3 times the records
  per tick at the start of the measured window. A fix lane is in progress.
- Foot placement: a rotating platform carries a pin by its velocity at the probe hit only (no turn). The planter has
  one ground sample per leg, so the lift-off floor ends at a fixed 5 cm from the lift point, and a platform that keeps
  moving during a release does not carry it.
- A `ChainIK` slot checks only the root and tip names: renaming an inner joint in place with per-joint limits keeps
  the limits resolved from the old names until the slot misses for another reason. Review findings 1-3 on the
