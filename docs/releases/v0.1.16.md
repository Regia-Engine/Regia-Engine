# v0.1.16 (patch on the alpha line, from v0.1.15): out-of-memory survival on small and integrated GPUs

Patch release for Direct Order 27, made on `release/0.1` (lane v0116-oom-survival). It answers the crash a DO27 tester
hit on an AMD Radeon 610M laptop (Vulkan on Windows, 5.7 GB of RAM): `wgpu out_of_memory error in stream_materials`,
then `GPU device lost`. This is stages 0 and 1 of the ultra-low-tier analysis. It is not the hard VRAM budget (stage 2)
and not the UltraLow preset (stage 4).

- **Commit:** v0.1.16 = the tag `v0.1.16` (the gated code commit is named in the tag message).
- **Workspace version:** set at release (this lane leaves the v0.1.15 value).
- **Branch:** `release/0.1`. `main` is the post-alpha development line (batch v020); this release is merged forward into it.

## Upgrade from v0.1.15
No host source change. No content format change. No new field on any public struct (`ExtractedMesh`, `FrameMesh`,
`TextureStreaming`, `PressureDecision`, `StreamReport` are unchanged). New API is additive: functions and constants
listed below, and one agent method. Host checklist: "Low-memory GPUs (v0.1.16)".

## What changes, on which machines

**Every GPU:**
- **The first out-of-memory error halves the texture streaming budget.** Before, only the second one within a minute
  did, and on the 610M the first was also the fatal one. Later errors step again when they come within 60 s of the
  previous one (at most 3 steps, never below 256 MiB), as before.
- **Streamed texture creation is caught.** Every streamed material texture is created inside a wgpu `OutOfMemory`
  error scope. A failure is counted (`gpu_health::captured_oom_count`), signals memory pressure, and never reaches the
  uncaptured-error handler. A material's first upload retries one mip level smaller until it fits. A growth step is
  dropped (the material keeps what it had). Streaming then backs off: no uploads and no eager first use for 5 s,
  doubling to at most 60 s on repeated failures.
- **A mip hold when pressure persists.** An out-of-memory error less than 60 s after a trim (from the VRAM probe, the
  OS, or another error) holds streamed textures one mip lower for 60 s: every streamed set gives up its top level
  once, the ones in view included, never below its floor. The textures grow back by need when the hold ends.
  Textures look softer during the hold.
- **No empty local shadow atlas.** With `local_shadow_max_lights = 0` (DO27's Low), casting lights no longer allocate
  the 4096² depth atlas (64 MiB) with no tile in it. With shadowed lights the atlas is the same as before.

**Integrated (shared-memory) GPUs on desktop only** (wgpu `IntegratedGpu`; not the Steam Deck, not Android):
- The texture streaming budget is at most **384 MiB**, never more than the device-local heap or a quarter of system
  RAM, and first use uploads the low mips first (`eager` off). On the 610M at Desktop Low this is 384 MiB instead of
  1 GiB eager.
- The new-scene warm on a scene-less window (ADR-190) is **off by default**: it uploads a whole map at once.

Discrete GPUs: no default change. Their streaming budgets, eager first use and scene warm are as in v0.1.15. Only a
real out-of-memory error changes anything (the first now halves the budget; one soon after a trim starts the hold).

## New switches
- `FERRITE_INTEGRATED_LIMITS=0`: turn the integrated-GPU defaults off (budget, eager, scene warm), as before v0.1.16.
- `FERRITE_SCENE_WARM=1`: keep the new-scene warm on an integrated GPU. (`FERRITE_SCENE_WARM=0` still turns it off
  everywhere.)

## New telemetry fields (additive)
- 10 s summary (`ferrite::frame_summary`), under `memory`:
  - `categories`: bytes per bucket at the last probe sample: `meshes`, `textures`, `virtual_texture`, `terrain`,
    `staging`, `atlases`, `shadows`, `render_targets`, `other`, plus `pipeline_cache` (RAM, the persistent pipeline
    cache size). `null` before the first sample, or without an allocator report.
  - `pressure`: `signals`, `texture_budget_steps`, `oom_uncaught`, `oom_caught`, `mips_held`, `mip_drops`,
    `stream_backing_off`, `integrated_limits`.
- Device-lost crash bundle: `crash.txt` has a "GPU memory (MiB, last probe)" line with the same buckets and the
  out-of-memory state; `host.json` has `gpu_memory`, `gpu_oom_errors` and `texture_budget_steps`.
- `render.problems` `vram_pressure` message now also says how many errors were caught and whether the mips are held.

## Not in this patch, and why
- **wgpu's `MemoryBudgetThresholds::for_resource_creation`.** In wgpu 30 the threshold check runs inside the hal
  `create_buffer` that wgpu-core's staging buffers use, and a staging failure loses the device. A threshold would make
  `write_buffer` and `write_texture` lose the device sooner than the driver does. It waits for the stage-2 hard budget.
- **Staging, submit and mesh uploads are still fatal on out-of-memory** (wgpu-core loses the device there). The
  scene warm and the per-frame mesh uploads are not budgeted yet (stage 3). This patch lowers how often they are
  reached on integrated GPUs; it does not make them survivable.
- No hard VRAM budget from `VK_EXT_memory_budget` / DXGI (stage 2), no UltraLow preset (stage 4).
- The translucent shadow maps are not freed when their quality goes to 0 (B8 second half).

## Tests
See the lane report. Lavapipe only: `tests/stream_oom_survives.rs` (200 streamed materials; real device out-of-memory
errors from a legal TiB-class texture through the test hook; the device survives 300+ frames, the budget steps on the
first error, the failed set is drawn at a lower mip, streaming backs off and recovers, the hold drops a level of every
set and they grow back; a 16 MiB budget variant stays under 16 MiB with no error), `tests/local_lights.rs`
(`casting_lights_under_a_zero_light_cap_allocate_no_local_atlas`), `tests/memory_pressure.rs` (updated: the first OOM
steps), unit tests in `gpu_health`, `texture_stream`, `low_memory` and `memory`. Not verified on a real integrated GPU,
on Windows, or on Android.
