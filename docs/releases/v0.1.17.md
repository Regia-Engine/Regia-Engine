# v0.1.17 (patch on the alpha line): cheap shader for the own canopy

Lane `v0117-canopy-glass`, from v0.1.15. Other v0.1.17 lanes add their own sections to this file.

## Own canopy glass (viewer-inside) (ADR-224)

**A visible change, on by default.** The pilot's own canopy (`GlassView::viewer_inside()`) no longer shows highlights
from cockpit lights, nav lights or emissive panels on the inside of the glass. Everything else about it is unchanged:
the sky and sun reflection, cracks, tint and absorption. `FERRITE_CANOPY_FULL=1` (or
`set_canopy_glass(CanopyGlass { shading: CanopyShading::Full, ..CanopyGlass::default() })`) restores the old look.
All other glass keeps the full shader.

What DO27 found (match logs of 2026-10-17, analysed in `<scratch>`): the
forward pass cost 8.5 to 13.3 ms in the cockpit against 1.8 to 2.9 ms on foot at the same render scale, on NVIDIA and
AMD, growing by about 4 ms per internal megapixel. Confirmed in the code: the own canopy covers most of the screen,
often in two layers, and its own-light pass ran the full forward shader (`shade_forward`): the clustered light loop
over the cockpit's lights and fitted emitters next to the eye, the sun's PCSS filter and near tile, probe GI, fog and
the occlusion terms, for every layer. Not yet measured: how much of the cockpit cost is the canopy rather than the
cockpit interior. The lead's real-GPU A/B (below) settles it.

Now (`ferrite_render`):
- A cheap entry point for viewer-inside glass (`lab_forward.wgsl` `shade_canopy`, `fs_canopy_add`,
  `fs_canopy_add_front`): the environment reflection with Fresnel (split sum), the sun's specular and diffuse behind one
  2×2 compare of the nearest sun cascade, planet-shine, the flat or interior ambient, crack shading and the material's
  emission. No local lights, no fitted emitters, no probe GI, no fog, no terrain or large-mesh terms. The multiply pass
  (tint and absorption) is the same. `shade_forward` is untouched: exterior glass and every other material draw
  exactly as before.
- Two more pipelines per glass set, built with the others: the game-frame warm and `prewarm_glass_instances` build
  them, and switching cheap and full mid-play compiles nothing.
- Settings: `ferrite_render::glass::CanopyGlass { shading, nearest_layer_only }` (default cheap, false), process-wide
  `set_canopy_glass` / `canopy_glass`, per renderer `FrameRenderer::set_canopy_glass(Option<_>)`, and
  `FERRITE_CANOPY_FULL=1` over both. `nearest_layer_only` (opt-in, frames with screen-space refraction only): a canopy
  layer behind the nearest one adds no light of its own.
- Telemetry: `gpu_pass_ms.forward_glass` (`GpuTimings::forward_glass_ms`), the forward pass's translucent section:
  every glass pane, the own canopy included, and the other blended draws. 0 without in-pass timestamps.
- Agent: `render.canopy_glass`, `render.set_canopy_glass`. Book: `book/agents/glass.md` § Own-canopy shading;
  `book/user-guide/do27-host-checklist.md` § Own canopy glass (viewer-inside).

Upgrade: no host source change. No host struct gained a field except `GpuTimings` (an output; a struct literal of it
needs `..Default::default()`). No content format change.

Tests (lavapipe, `crates/ferrite_render/tests/canopy_glass.rs`, a synthetic cockpit: a 1 m glass sphere cap around the
camera, three point lights, two emissive panels and another ship's refracting canopy outside it, sun and sky IBL):
- `the_cheap_canopy_stays_close_to_the_full_shader`: lavapipe, mean 5071.8 cd/m² full vs 5071.65 cheap, mean |diff| 0.01 %, max |diff| 6.9 cd/m²
- `nearest_layer_only_drops_the_inner_sheets_light_and_stays_lit`: lavapipe, two sheets 3 cm apart: both layers 4881.2 cd/m², nearest only 4383.9 (the dropped sheet is 10.19 % of the mean; bound 20 %)
- `the_process_wide_setting_and_the_renderer_override`: the default draws the cheap path, `set_canopy_glass(Full)`
  draws the full shader bit for bit, a renderer override beats the process-wide value (lavapipe, passed).
- `the_warm_builds_the_canopy_pipelines`: lavapipe, refraction off and on: the canopy frames create 6 pipelines cold
  and 0 after `prewarm_glass_instances`.

Results measured 2026-10-18 on lavapipe (the build server) on the canopy-hardening lane (ADR-262), whose shader changes leave
these frames unchanged: the first test read 5071.78 / 5071.80 cd/m² full and 5071.63 / 5071.65 cheap (refraction off /
on), the same as above. The hardening tests are in `docs/releases/v0.1.21-canopy-hardening.md`.
