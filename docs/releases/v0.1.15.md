# v0.1.15 (patch on the alpha line, from v0.1.13.1): skinned motion vectors and warm gaps

Two changes, previously drafted as v0.1.15 notes (the telemetry patch took v0.1.15).

## Skinned motion vectors


Patch release for Direct Order 27's alpha build, made on `release/0.1`. It fixes the blur DO27's playtest found on
moving characters: skinned meshes (3P pilots, Steve) wrote no motion vectors, so TAA and TSR smeared them on every
tier while rigid hulls in the same shot stayed sharp.

- **Commit:** v0.1.15 = the tag `v0.1.15` (the gated code commit is named in the tag message).
- **Workspace version:** `0.1.14` (set by the release commit).
- **Branch:** `release/0.1` (alpha hotfixes). `main` is the post-alpha development line (batch v020); this release is merged forward into it.

## Upgrade from v0.1.13
No host source change and no new API. `FrameMesh`, `ExtractedMesh` and the render settings are unchanged; there is no
new setting. Behaviour changes: multi-joint skinned meshes write motion vectors (TAA, TSR, FSR 3 and motion blur read
them); still frames are unchanged. Keep a character's `FrameMesh::object_key` stable across frames, as for rigid motion
(the renderer keys the previous palette by it). Book: `book/user-guide/do27-host-checklist.md` § Skinned motion
vectors (v0.1.15).

## Skinned meshes write motion vectors (ADR-221)
What DO27 found (`MOVING_UNIT_BLUR_report.md`, "Exact report for Ferrite"), confirmed in the code:
- The velocity draw list took `draw.rigid` draws only (`rigid` = an empty joint palette), so a skinned mesh drew no
  motion. With `depth_velocity` (every shipping preset) its pixels got the camera's motion only; without it, the
  cleared (0, 0).
- The renderer kept last frame's model matrix per object, but no joint palette; `velocity.wgsl` never skinned (the
  joint attributes were read only to keep them alive); `SkinPalette::prev_joints` was never read.
- ADR-183's one-joint fold (v0.1.5) already gave single-joint parts (canopies, fans, doors) motion vectors through
  the rigid path. Characters have many joints and kept the skinned path.

Now (`ferrite_render`):
- The renderer keeps last frame's packed palette and each skinned object's span in it, next to the previous model
  matrices: rebuilt from the objects drawn each frame, cleared on a camera cut, kept across reflection-probe captures
  and separately per texture view.
- Skinned opaque draws join the velocity pass. A new vertex entry, `vs_skinned`, skins each vertex with this frame's
  palette and with last frame's (the same linear-blend skinning as the forward pass), applies the current and previous
  model matrices, and writes `previous uv − current uv` like the rigid path. Instances of one skinned mesh batch into
  one instanced draw, each with its own palettes.
- No previous palette (an object's first frame, a frame after it was not drawn, a camera cut, a changed joint count):
  the current palette stands in, so the limbs write no motion that frame and the model matrix still moves the object.
- With `depth_velocity`, a skinned object that moved neither its model nor any joint is skipped, like a still rigid
  mesh.
- The two new velocity pipelines (back-face and cull-none) are built with the existing ones, so the warm builds them
  and a character met later compiles none. All velocity pipelines are shared per device (`DevicePipelines`), so a
  texture view with TAA on, or a stand-in renderer, compiles none on its first frame.
- The velocity joint buffer starts at 128 KiB: nine 80-joint or three 256-joint moving instances fit without growing
  it mid-play (more grow it once, rebuilding one bind group).
- A skinned mesh whose material graph has a vertex stage writes its skinned motion without the graph's offset.

Tests (`crates/ferrite_render/tests/skinned_velocity.rs`, lavapipe; `do27_joint_counts_*` cover 67 joints animated
and static, 80, 256, and the joint buffer growing):
- `skinned_joints_write_motion_{per_mesh_velocity,depth_velocity}`: a two-joint mesh whose joint 1 moves 0.3 m
  writes −0.0776 UV over all 660 pixels of that limb (expected −0.3 / 3.87 m = −0.0775) and 0 on the unmoved limb; a
  0.15 m move writes −0.0388. A second instance of the same mesh (same batch, its own palette) stays at 0. Zero motion
  on the first frame, a still frame, a grown palette (3 joints), a reappearance after a frame away, and after a camera
  cut. A rigid bar in the same frames writes bit-identical motion with and without the skinned meshes beside it.
- `tsr_keeps_a_moving_skinned_card_as_sharp_as_a_rigid_one`: a 4 px striped card slides 2.5 px a frame for 16 frames
  under TSR, once moved by a two-joint palette and once by its model matrix. Fixed: both resolve identically (stripe
  energy 0.744 of native for both, mean error from native 11.17 luma for both). With the fix disabled the skinned
  card was 58.0 luma from native (rigid: 11.2) and its ghost raised its stripe energy to 2.29× native.
- Goldens unchanged: `ferrite_test_utils` (all, including `golden_skinned_fox`, `gpu_matches_cpu`, `taau_golden`,
  `lens_goldens`, with the Khronos assets) and `lookdev`'s `default_look_golden`. TAA/TSR/FSR 3 motion tests
  (`taa_motion`, `tsr_motion`, `fsr3_motion`, `black_sky_motion`, `depth_velocity`, `doublesided_gaps`,
  `camera_cut`, `rigid_skin_fold`) pass.
- `lookdev` `warm_coverage`: 0 pipelines outside the warm queue and 0 on the frame thread from the first game frame.

## Known
- Not measured on DO27's content yet. Check on the alpha maps: the pilot walking in 3P with TSR on keeps its
  detail; compare `DO27_QUALITY_OFF=taau`.
- Each moving skinned instance copies its two palettes into the velocity pass's joint buffer every frame (changed
  ranges only; about 6 KiB for a 64-joint character). Not timed on a GPU in this lane (lavapipe only).
- Translucent (blended) skinned meshes write no motion, as for rigid ones.
- Flaky on a loaded build host (passes alone): `upload_queue::tests::a_slow_frame_stops_at_the_time_budget` (a 1 µs
  budget assumes every 16 KiB queue write takes longer).

## Warm coverage gaps


Patch release for Direct Order 27's alpha build, made on `release/0.1` from v0.1.13 (lane v0113-warm-gaps2). It
closes the engine side of the warm-coverage gaps DO27's rehearsal B found on the packaged v0.1.11 build: pipelines

- **Workspace version:** set at release (this lane leaves `0.1.13`).
- **Branch:** `release/0.1` (alpha hotfixes). `main` is the post-alpha development line (batch v020); this release is merged forward into it.

## Warm coverage: device-level pipelines and what play switches on (ADR-220)
The sites in the rehearsal, read at v0.1.11:

| Site (v0.1.11) | Pipeline | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| `frame.rs:12575` | `cluster pipeline` (compute) | `build_pipes` of a renderer that had none |
| `frame.rs:12625` | `page clear` | the same `build_pipes` |
| `frame/translucent_shadow.rs:198` | translucent casters, plain and `GLASS`, culled and cull-none (8) | the same `build_pipes` (`TranslucentPipes::new`) |
| `tsr/mod.rs:689` | `tsr prepare`, `tsr resolve`, `tsr sharpen` | the renderer's first TSR frame |
| `frame.rs:2483` | `depth match` | the first scaled (or MSAA) frame with effect layers, and every new output size |
| `outdoor.rs:1922` | `outdoor sky/fog/clouds` (`cached_pipe`) | the first frame of a map with clouds or froxel fog after a warm on a map without |

- **`build_pipes` outside the warm:** these are the pipelines a renderer builds itself when it builds its pipeline
  state. The warmed runtime renderer already has them, so another renderer built them. No forward-set site
  (`frame.rs` 12929 and its neighbours) was logged with them, so that renderer drew no frame: it only prewarmed. From
  the code, the path that fits is `GameFrameWarm` holding the runtime renderer on a worker and leaving a stand-in,
  while a graph material resolves: `ferrite_matgraph`'s `prewarm_graph_pipelines` calls `prewarm_material_graphs` on
  the stand-in, and that builds the stand-in's pipes on the main thread. A host `prewarm` on the stand-in would do the
  same. DO27 times against its own `first_gameplay_frame`, which comes before the engine's (ADR-210). The rehearsal
  report lists sites, not log lines, so this is an inference; the fix does not depend on which renderer it was.
- **TSR and the depth match, 5–15 s into play:** the presets are native + TAAU, and DO27 applies its 0.67 + TSR lever
  after a `SettingsChanged` of `graphics.preset`. On a fresh HOME the first-launch preset re-seeds the profile, so the
  warm frame and the first frames of play can draw native TAAU, and the first TSR frame compiles TSR and, at the
  lower scale, the depth match for the effects. The depth match was also rebuilt, pipeline included, at every new
  output size (a fullscreen toggle).
- **The outdoor passes on a second map:** their pipelines were built when a frame first drew them. Bore Twelve's warm
  frame has no cloud layer or froxel fog. The next map's first frame compiled them, with no warm of its own (DO27's
  shell warms once per session; its WARM_PER_MAP lane fixes that side).

Now:
- The cluster pipeline, the page clear, the translucent casters, the three TSR pipelines, the depth match and the
  outdoor sky, fog and cloud pipelines belong to the device (`RenderDevice::shared_pipelines`, ADR-195). A stand-in,
  a second renderer, a texture view's own TSR pass and a new output size compile none of them once a warm has.
- Game-frame warms (`warm_extracted_at` with a window size) also build what play can switch on: TSR, the depth
  match (single-sample and MSAA sources, whatever the warm frame used), every outdoor pass for the HDR targets the warm drew (sky and aerial
  perspective; froxel fog and clouds where the profile has them), TAAU's resolve and sharpen when the warm frame used
  TSR, and the half-resolution SSGI upsample (Desktop High at exactly 0.67).
- The lab's fog occluder (a test fixture no game frame draws) is no longer built with every renderer's outdoor state.
- `samples/lookdev/tests/warm_coverage.rs` now switches to 0.67 + TSR at step 70, prewarms a graph on a stand-in at
  75, goes fullscreen at 80, and loads the next map with clouds and fog at 90 (no second warm). A third test plays a
  TSR session over two maps with a warm per map and a stand-in graph prewarm during the second warm. Both assert **0**
  outside the warm queue and 0 on the host thread from the first game frame on. Results: see "Test results" below.

## Test results
Lavapipe on the test machine:
- Before the fix (the test commit alone), `warm_coverage` failed 2 of 3. The session had 21 outside the warm queue and 4 on
  the frame thread; the TSR session over two maps had 10 and 2. The sites were DO27's: `frame.rs:12575`, `:12625`,
  `translucent_shadow.rs:198`, `tsr/mod.rs:689`, `frame.rs:2483`, `outdoor.rs:1922`.
- With the fix: 3 of 3 pass. The session has 0 outside and 0 on the frame thread (warm: 89 pipelines). The TSR session
  over two maps has 0 and 0 (warms: 84 pipelines, then 0).
- `ferrite_render` lib: 698 passed (with the new `a_second_renderer_and_a_resize_take_the_device_level_pipelines`).
  Clippy `-D warnings` is clean for `ferrite_render` and `lookdev`.
- The Android check was not run on the lane (no NDK on the rig).

## Robustness
- The mid-play step of a game-frame warm is optional: if it fails, the warm logs a warning and continues (window-size
  frame, level graphs, camera cut), so the renderer never comes back with the 64x64 warm frame's history. Only the
  shutdown cancellation still ends the warm early.

## Not covered
- FSR 3 switched on mid-play (its pipelines are the renderer's own; not DO27's default).
- MSAA switched on mid-play after a single-sample warm frame: the MSAA depth match is warmed; the MSAA forward sets
  are the renderer's own and still build on the first MSAA frame.
- A `hdr_target` change mid-play (Rg11B10 vs Rgba16F), or a map with outdoor passes when the warm frame's map has
  none: the outdoor pipelines are warmed only for the formats and fog/cloud combinations the warm frame drew.
- Pipelines of a renderer that takes over after an abandoned warm (ADR-196): the forward sets are the renderer's own.
- The first probe capture of a map made before the game-frame warm (unchanged since v0.1.11, behind the loading
  screen).

## Known
- Flaky under load on the gate box (pass alone): `resize_memory`, `device_lost_exit[surface]`.
