## Spec 56: dialogue, captions, subtitles
- Verdict: fixed and ready to build.
- Set ids compared: runtime-media/runtime-media-subtitles, developer-tools/developer-tools-localization (both
  opened; no hazard file, no CLEANROOM-DENY path, no generic-game-engine-specs path opened).
- Changes: baseline marked (built `regia_ui::subtitles::SubtitleQueue`, `ui.subtitles`, ADR-1570 on integ/ext2);
  new types renamed to avoid the built and spec-36 names (`CaptionBoard`, `CaptionOptions`, `conversation` format
  `.conv.ron`, `conversation.*`, `captions.*`, `conv_*` codes, copied wrapping vocabulary replaced by `keep_breaks`); shared messages
  moved from `regia_core` (no ECS there) to `regia_app::caption`; conversation runner moved from `regia_audio`
  to `regia_framework` (layer rule: audio and ui are siblings); PO exchange section dropped in favor of spec 36 § 3;
  `CUE_VERSION` 2 -> 3 now requires a migrator and `upgrade-project` step; fallback chain is a free function.
- Open (lead): specs 36 and 56 overlap (both define dialogue assets, a subtitle manager and `dialogue.*`/`subtitles.*`
  methods). 56 now uses disjoint names and states the boundary in § 1.1; the lead should confirm or merge them.
