The engine's source code is under the Mozilla Public License 2.0, which does not grant any rights to these names or
the logo (MPL-2.0 § 2.3). This page says how you may use them.

## Allowed without asking

- **"Made with Regia Engine"** (or "Built with Regia Engine", "Powered by Regia Engine") in your game's credits,
  about box, store page, trailer or website, with or without the logo, for a game or app built on Regia Engine.
  `regia package` adds the line "Made with Regia Engine" to a package's credits by default; you may keep it, move it
  or remove it (`--no-engine-credit`, `[package] engine_credit = false`). Crediting is welcome, never required.
- **Redistributing unmodified releases** of Regia Engine under its name, for example mirroring a release archive or
  packaging it for an operating system, as long as the content is the release as published.
- **Talking about Regia Engine**: articles, videos, courses, books, talks, forum posts, comparisons and reviews may
  use the name to refer to the engine.

## Not allowed

- **A modified distribution shipped as a product must use another name.** If you change the engine and ship that
  engine (an editor, an SDK, a fork) to others, give it a name of your own that does not contain "Regia". You may say
  truthfully that it is "based on Regia Engine". A game built on your changed engine is not an engine distribution:
  the credit line above is fine.
- **No implied endorsement.** Do not use the names or the logo in a way that suggests Sphenisca Regia LLC
  sponsors, certifies or endorses you, your product or your company, unless it has agreed in writing.
- **No confusing names.** Do not use "Regia" or a confusingly similar name as the name of your own engine, tool,
  company, domain or account.
- **Do not alter the logo** beyond scaling it and the color variants published with it.

## Questions

Ask the maintainers if you are unsure whether a use is allowed. This policy may be updated; the version in the
current release applies.
